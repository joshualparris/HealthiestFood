Does your phone keep the time?

This is an optional, on-device reliability test, not a background alarm. Most phones suspend browser JavaScript with the screen locked. The countdown uses an absolute clock deadline so that, when you return, the time shown can catch up. Audible notifications while locked are not guaranteed.

Actual phone check

  1. On Android Chrome or iPhone Safari, start both test timers above.
  2. Switch to another tab. Reopen after the first timer should finish. Record what you observe.
  3. Repeat by starting again, locking the phone for at least 100 seconds, then unlocking and reopening the same page.
  4. Confirm both timers show finished. Also note whether your phone actually alerted you while locked, not simply when reopened.
  5. Repeat with the browser closed and reopened; saved deadlines can survive refresh but private browsing or clearing storage may remove them.

For cooking: use the phone's Clock app or a physical kitchen timer for cooking, cooling or food-safety deadlines. Keep the device charged. A browser screen-wake request may prevent automatic screen dimming while visible, but is not a background alarm.
