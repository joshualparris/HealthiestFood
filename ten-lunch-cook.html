Complete cooking instructions — works even on older devices

These steps remain available if this device cannot run the interactive cooking guide. You can scroll through them without any JavaScript.

0 · Set up your kitchen

Get ten clean shallow containers and space in the fridge. Wash produce. Check every pack's ingredients, especially milk/cross-contact. If cooling 10 portions won't fit safely, cook five tonight and five later.

  1. Lay out 10 freezer-safe containers. Label five A1–A5 and five B1–B5.
  2. Weigh ingredients before cooking. Recipe A and B below show the split amounts.
  3. Wash and chop vegetables; keep the raw fish-free and tofu preparations clean.

1 · Start grains and lentils

Parallel cooking saves time. Follow each packet for water ratios, and keep cooked rice out of prolonged room-temperature holding.

  1. Cook 200 g dry brown rice in fresh water according to the packet (often 25–40 minutes).
  2. Rinse and cook 150 g dry quinoa according to the packet (often 12–15 minutes).
  3. Rinse 150 g red lentils; simmer 10–15 minutes until tender. Drain excess liquid. Save the remaining 150 g for curry.

2 · Make 5 salmon bowls (A1–A5)

Fresh greens and broccolini can replace the frozen vegetables by weight. This is the full amount for this batch only.

  1. In 20 mL olive oil cook 150 g onion + 250 g carrot for 6–10 min; add 200 g capsicum, 10 g garlic, 1 tsp cumin and 2 tsp paprika.
  2. Add 400 g passata, 400 g broccoli/broccolini and 200 g spinach/bok choy leaves. Simmer until cooked and sauce thickens.
  3. Mix in the cooked 150 g quinoa and 150 g lentils. Fold in both salmon tins (drained as appropriate); mash any soft edible bones. Heat through, finish with ~30 mL lemon juice.
  4. Weigh finished fish batch and divide evenly among A1–A5. Do not assume a fixed cooked weight.

3 · Make 5 tofu bowls (B1–B5)

Cook the plant-based curry until pumpkin and lentils are tender and the tofu is heated through.

  1. In 20 mL olive oil soften 200 g onion + 200 g carrot; add 10 g garlic, 2 tsp curry powder, 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp turmeric.
  2. Add 400 g prepared pumpkin, 150 g dry rinsed lentils, 300 g passata and 300–500 mL water. Simmer, stirring, ~20–30 minutes until tender.
  3. Add 450 g diced firm tofu, 1 drained/rinsed chickpea tin, 300 g broccoli and 200 g greens. Cook ~8–10 minutes until hot throughout; add ~30 mL lemon juice.
  4. Portion the cooked 200 g dry-equivalent brown rice evenly into B1–B5. Divide finished curry evenly over rice by weight.

4 · Chill, freeze and pack

Food safety is more important than ticking every box. Refrigerate promptly; do not leave fish/rice/egg/lentil meals on the bench for hours.

  1. Divide food into shallow containers as cooking finishes. Vent steam briefly; don't stack hot full containers.
  2. Refrigerate promptly and within 2 hours of cooking. Use fridge space so food can cool rapidly; work in smaller batches if necessary.
  3. Once thoroughly chilled, close and freeze around −18°C. Label contents and cooking date; freeze the rest if not eating within 2 days.
  4. At work, keep meal chilled/frozen in transit and microwave, stirring, until steaming hot throughout.
