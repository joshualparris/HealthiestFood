Your ten-lunch cook-up.

One calm cooking sequence

Before starting: clear enough fridge space for ten shallow containers. If not, cook just five tonight and the other five on another day. A ticked box is only a checklist, not a temperature measurement.

A · 5 salmon bowls

Cooked from 150 g dry quinoa + 150 g lentils, two 415 g salmon tins, 400 g broccoli, 200 g greens, 250 g carrot, 150 g onion, 200 g capsicum, 400 g passata, 20 mL oil, 10 g garlic, 30 mL lemon juice and spices.

B · 5 tofu curries

200 g dry brown rice + 150 g dry lentils, 450 g tofu, one chickpea tin, 400 g prepared pumpkin, 300 g broccoli, 200 g greens, 200 g carrot, 200 g onion, 300 g passata, 20 mL oil, 10 g garlic, 30 mL lemon juice and spices.

Label and store your lunches

Safety: promptly divide and chill cooked food in shallow portions, refrigerate within two hours, and freeze after chilling. Keep the fridge at 5°C or below and freezer around −18°C. Reheat until steaming hot throughout; stir to avoid cold spots. Do not use this checklist to override actual temperature checks or the NSW Food Authority leftovers guidance.
