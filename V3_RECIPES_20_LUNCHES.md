# V3 — 20 evidence-based Woolworths freezer lunches (Dubbo NSW)

**Version:** 3.0 • **Researched:** 16 October 2026 • **Status:** proposed, not cooked, tasted, lab-analysed or checked out at Dubbo Woolworths.  
**Recipes:** 10 Mediterranean oily-fish/bean/quinoa bowls + 10 ginger–turmeric lentil/pea/tofu/brown-rice bowls. Dairy-free; gluten-free **only once actual purchased labels/cross-contact are checked**. Adult lunches, not a prescription or a complete 24-hour diet.

> **No “absolute healthiest meal” or 100/100 longevity score exists.** V3 is more precise, varied and nutritionally robust **as a planned rotation**, not proven superior to V2 in trials. See [evidence audit](V3_NUTRITION_EVIDENCE_AND_SCORES.md), [itemised prices + basket](V3_WOOLWORTHS_DUBBO_SHOPPING.md), and historical [V2](OPTIMISED_20_LUNCHES_2026-10-16.md).

## V3 changes from V2 and why they matter

1. **Two genuinely different fruit exposures**, oranges with A, green kiwifruit with B. Whole fruit helps vitamin-C availability with plant iron; variety is preferable to claiming oranges are uniquely best.
2. **Measured nut and seed options:** A adds 15 g walnuts; B adds 10 g walnuts **and** 10 g unsalted pumpkin kernels (pepitas) per bowl. These add complementary plant fats and minerals, not guaranteed life extension. These are energy-dense; reduce if meals are too large.
3. **Calcium checked on the actual product:** optional 250 mL **Sanitarium So Good High Protein SOY** per lunch (~400 mg calcium, 10 g protein, fortified B12/D on listed label). It is *not* literally unsweetened; contains small amounts of cane sugar and maltodextrin. Do not count Macro Firm Tofu's calcium without a numeric label (firming agent 511 = magnesium chloride).
4. **Use exact Woolworths label sources, not category guesses:** frozen baby peas are peas-only and shell-free; SPC tomato is 100% tomato; Macro tofu 14.8 g protein/100 g; frozen broccoli/cauliflower is only those vegetables. Product labels, prices and stock can change.
5. **Better buying maths:** edible/drained weights; 2 × 190 g walnuts not one; 4 × 400 g tomatoes not two; 5 × 1 L fortified milk if taken with all lunches; *actual* seed, fruit, garlic, lentil and tofu quantities.
6. **Consistency and safe handling:** alternate A and B across all four weeks, portion shallowly, refrigerate/freeze within two hours and reheat rice just once. Skip the urge to make all twenty hot meals in one session if cold-storage capacity is limited.
7. **No unproven “superfood” inflation:** no collagen, MCT, seaweed/kelp, exotic extracts or megadose supplements. Iodine is a *whole-day* concern; use iodised salt sparingly **only if food needs salt**.

### Schedule for 20 weekday lunches

Week 1: A B A B A. Week 2: B A B A B. Repeat weeks 1–2 for weeks 3–4. This yields **10 A and 10 B meals** with 2–3 fish lunches in each five-workday week.

**Fish arithmetic:** 12 × 110 g sardine tins at **~75 g drained/tin** = **~900 g** sardines → **eight ~112.5 g portions**. One 210 g pink salmon tin at **~166 g drained** = **two ~83 g portions**. Total **~1,066 g drained per four weeks**, averaging **~267 g/week**, close to 2–3 standard 100 g tinned fish serves weekly; actual edible weights must be checked and the 83 g salmon portions alone are less than a standard fish serving. Fish eaten outside lunch also counts toward the guideline. Individual fish products have different omega-3 amounts; don't infer a numerical EPA/DHA total from gross can weight.

## Recipe A — V3 Mediterranean sardine/salmon/bean/quinoa bowls (10 lunches)

### Ingredients — weighed and sourced

**One ten-portion shared base**
- **300 g dry** plain quinoa (existing Coles Wellness Road 100% quinoa is fine; at Woolworths choose a single-ingredient quinoa product).
- **2 × 420 g tins** Woolworths No Added Salt Chickpeas; **1 × 420 g tin** Woolworths Black Beans (look for no-added-salt where possible); **1 × 420 g tin** No Added Salt Red Kidney Beans; **drain, rinse and use all drained beans**. Check allergy warnings.
- **2 × 400 g tins** [SPC Diced Tomatoes](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/322960), 100% tomatoes, no added salt/sugar.
- **400 g peeled** sweet potato (~500–600 g to purchase, depends on peel), **400 g carrot**, **300 g edible red capsicum**, **300 g zucchini**, **250 g peeled brown onion**.
- **2 × 500 g** [Woolworths Australian Frozen Broccoli & Cauliflower](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/768561) = 1,000 g; **280 g Woolworths baby leaf spinach**.
- **5 minced garlic cloves**, juice of **2 lemons**, **60 mL extra virgin olive oil** (35 mL roast, 15 mL bean sauce, 10 mL finish), **2 tbsp ground cumin**, **2 tbsp smoked paprika**, **2 tsp dried oregano**, **1 tsp ground black pepper**. Optional iodised salt **no more than ½ tsp total**, taste before adding.
- **Fish**: **12 × 110 g John West Sardines in Springwater** (label approximately 75 g drained per tin) + **1 × 210 g Woolworths Pink Salmon with edible soft bones** (~166 g drained). Use exact product label for sodium/gluten.
- **At eating, not frozen in bowls:** **15 g unsalted walnuts per lunch** = **150 g per A batch**; **10 whole medium oranges**; and optionally **250 mL high-protein calcium-fortified soy drink per lunch** if it fits the rest of your day's calcium needs.

### Method — 60–90 minutes plus safe chilling
1. Prepare 10 **shallow** 750–1,000 mL freezer-safe containers and clean scales. Heat fan oven to **200°C**. Dice potatoes ~2 cm; carrots ~1 cm, capsicum and zucchini ~2 cm; onions ~1 cm.
2. Mix sweet potato, carrots, capsicum, zucchini and half the onion with **35 mL EVOO**, **1 tbsp cumin**, **1 tbsp smoked paprika** and some of the black pepper. Spread uncrowded across 2–3 trays. Roast **30–40 minutes**, turn halfway, until soft with browned edges.
3. Rinse **300 g dry quinoa**. Cook according to the specific packet, typically in about **600 mL water** for 15–18 minutes; stand covered 5 min; fluff.
4. Heat **15 mL EVOO** in a large pot. Soften remaining onion 5–7 min, then minced garlic 1 min. Add remaining 1 tbsp each cumin and paprika, oregano and remaining pepper; cook **30–60 seconds**. Stir in **four drained/rinsed bean tins**, **two tomato tins**, and ~300 mL water; simmer **10–15 minutes**.
5. Cook the frozen broccoli/cauliflower **fully according to pack directions**. Fold it, roasted veg, spinach, quinoa, lemon juice and remaining **10 mL EVOO** into sauce. Check taste, only then consider optional iodised salt.
6. Weigh the finished cooked **shared base**, divide its weight by **10**, and weigh that mass into each container. The base includes all vegetables, beans and grain, *but not fish*.
7. Drain all 12 sardine tins. Weigh the combined edible fish and divide equally into **8 piles** (if 900 g, **112.5 g per pile**). Place one pile in bowls A1–A8. Drain the one salmon tin, weigh and split into **2 piles** (if 166 g, **83 g**), add to A9/A10. Keep edible soft bones. If strongly averse to freezing/reheating sardines at work, safely store sealed tins separately and add after reheating the base: **do not freeze unopened tins**.
8. Divide into shallow containers and **refrigerate or freeze within 2 hours of cooking**, using space for good airflow. Don't stack hot portions in a warm pile.
9. At work, thaw in fridge when practical and reheat a single portion **until steaming hot throughout**, stirring halfway. Add **15 g walnuts after heating** and eat an orange. Soy milk is a companion, not an ingredient to freeze.

## Recipe B — V3 ginger–turmeric lentil/tofu/pea & greens bowls (10 lunches)

### Ingredients — weighed and sourced

- **400 g dry brown/green whole lentils** (from a 500 g bag; inspect for gluten grain cross-contact if strict GF).
- **300 g dry** wholegrain brown rice (from a 1 kg bag; choose [SunRice Australian Brown Medium Grain](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/94536) or one-ingredient equivalent).
- **1 × 1 kg** [Woolworths Australian Baby Peas](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/754181), ingredients **100% peas**, not pods. **Do not** use uncertain 1 kg "edamame pods" as if all were edible shelled soybeans.
- **3 × 450 g** [Macro Firm Tofu](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/23038) = **1,350 g**; raw tofu **135 g per bowl**.
- **2 × 400 g tins** SPC Diced Tomatoes (no added salt/sugar); **600 g peeled** butternut pumpkin (buy ~800–900 g unpeeled), **400 g carrots**, **400 g cup mushrooms**, **250 g peeled red onions**, **200 g de-stemmed kale leaves** (buy 1–2 bunches depending on usable yield), **1 × 500 g** plain frozen broccoli/cauliflower.
- **6 garlic cloves**, **20 g peeled ginger**, juice of **2 lemons**; **50 mL EVOO** (30 mL roast veg; 10 mL tofu; 10 mL sauce); **2 tbsp cumin**, **1 tbsp ground coriander**, **2 tsp turmeric**, **1 tsp smoked paprika**, **1 tsp black pepper**; optional iodised salt **≤½ tsp total**, after tasting.
- **At eating:** **10 g walnuts per lunch** (=100 g), **10 g Woolworths plain unsalted pumpkin kernels/pepitas per lunch** (=100 g); **10 whole green kiwifruit**; optionally 250 mL high-protein calcium-fortified soy milk per lunch.

### Method — 75–100 minutes plus safe chilling
1. Preheat fan oven **200°C**. Wash/peel/dice pumpkin into ~2 cm cubes and slice carrots ~1 cm; halve or quarter mushrooms. Roast pumpkin, carrot, mushrooms with **30 mL EVOO**, 1 tbsp cumin and paprika on uncrowded trays for **25–35 min**.
2. Cut all **1,350 g tofu** into 2 cm cubes. On separate uncrowded trays toss with **10 mL EVOO** and a small spoon of the measured seasonings; roast **20–25 min**, until heated and lightly browned (times depend on oven/tray crowding).
3. Wash **300 g dry brown rice**; cook in water according to its packet, often **30–40 min**. Cook **400 g whole brown/green lentils** separately in abundant water until tender but intact (~20–30 min), then drain. Cooked rice **must be cooled rapidly** after cooking.
4. In large pan sauté red onion **5–7 min** in remaining **10 mL EVOO**. Stir in 6 cloves minced garlic and 20 g fresh ginger for ~1 min, then add remaining cumin, coriander, turmeric and pepper. Add 2 tomato tins and **~300 mL water**, simmer 10 min.
5. Cook all 1 kg baby peas and 500 g frozen broccoli/cauliflower **fully to their package directions**. Add de-stemmed kale to the sauce and wilt thoroughly for ~3–5 min; combine with other vegetables.
6. Mix cooked rice, lentils, tofu, sauce, roasted/frozen vegetables and juice of two lemons. Weigh the combined batch, divide into **10 equal cooked masses** in shallow meal containers.
7. Refrigerate/freeze promptly, with particular care for cooked rice (see safety). Reheat single servings just once until steaming hot. Top each bowl with **10 g walnuts + 10 g plain unsalted pepitas**, and eat **1 green kiwifruit**. Keep nuts/pepitas dry and separate until eating.

## Why V3 is more robust — and its limits

- **Not “more ingredients always better”:** the fruit/seed swaps modestly improve variety, while the large majority of benefit comes from regular veg, legumes, wholegrains, healthy fat and adequate protein. Extra seeds add calories, so skip or downsize if appetite/weight needs differ.
- **Marine omega-3** and bone calcium are available in oily fish; **ALA** is available from walnuts. These are not nutritionally interchangeable, which is why V3 retains both.
- A 250 mL serve of [Sanitarium So Good High Protein Soy](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/6021686) is labelled about **10 g protein and 400 mg calcium**; it is **gluten-free according to current listing** but **contains soy, cane sugar and maltodextrin**. It is an optional separate daily calcium-rich food. Do **not** use [Vitasoy Calci-Plus](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/153587) as GF: label includes **barley flour/malt and gluten**.
- [Macro firm tofu](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/23038) lists **14.8 g protein per 100 g** and is firmed with additive 511 (magnesium chloride). **Calcium is not quantified** in this listing. Its allergen label says **contains soy; may contain peanuts and sesame**.
- A strict gluten-free diet requires **checking the real tins and spice/lentil pack** for gluten and cross-contact warnings. Some brisling sardines (e.g. photographed Safcol) carry wheat/gluten precautionary warnings.
- **Iodine**, vitamin D, B12, iron and total calcium depend on your **whole day's food**. Don't add kelp or extra salt purely to force a target; consult food labels and appropriate professional advice if clinically needed.
- **Energy and nutrients are not certified.** Dry-to-cooked yield, fish drained weight, tofu/pea brands, fruit size, seeds and optional soy drinks make prior per-bowl kcal/protein/fibre numbers obsolete. See the transparent label-only calculation in the audit; **don't claim every lunch is 30 g protein without checking the base**.
- Meal prep food safety: cool in shallow containers, **refrigerate/freeze within two hours**, cooked rice/pasta refrigerated **max two days**, leftovers frozen **up to three months**, thaw in fridge and eat within 24 hours, reheat to steaming hot throughout; rice **never reheated more than once**. [NSW Food Authority](https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers).

## Evidence
- [Australian Dietary Guidelines](https://www.eatforhealth.gov.au/guidelines/australian-dietary-guidelines-1-5) (overall eating pattern, vegetables/beans, grains, suitable portions).
- [Heart Foundation omega fats/fish](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/healthy-living/healthy-eating/omega-3-omega-6-heart-health) (2–3 servings oily fish per week, plant ALA).
- [PREDIMED 2018 corrected randomised-trial report](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa1800389) (Mediterranean *pattern* reduced cardiovascular outcomes in people at high risk; **did not test these bowls**).
- [FSANZ iodine in foods](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/nutrition/iodine-sensitivities/iodinefood) (adult RDI 150 micrograms/day, variation across foods).
- [NSW Food Authority leftovers](https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers).

**Recipe goal:** sustainable, repeatable nutrient-dense lunches, not maximal calories, maximal fibre, or a fictitious "perfect 100" health score.
