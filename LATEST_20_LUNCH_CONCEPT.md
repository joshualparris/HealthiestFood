# Josh's 20 Freezer Lunches: Two Batches

*Gluten-free, dairy-free, built from Woolworths. Written 16 October 2026.*

**Batch A** is the Mediterranean sardine / salmon / tofu bowl (10 lunches). **Batch B** is a plant-forward lentil / edamame / greens bowl (10 lunches). Together they give you fish 6 times in 20 days, tofu and legumes every day, and two quite different flavour profiles.

## Read this first

- Neither recipe is provably "the healthiest possible". Both follow the pattern with the strongest evidence: legumes daily, lots of vegetable variety, oily fish a few times a week, intact grains, nuts, olive oil, low sodium.
- Nutrition per bowl is my estimate from the ingredients, not a lab figure. Expect roughly 500-650 kcal, 28-35 g protein and 16-20 g fibre per bowl.
- Prices are ballpark. Prices you gave me are marked (confirmed), the rest are my estimates. Check the Woolworths app for your Dubbo store.
- Check every label for gluten, especially soy milk, spices and any tinned item.
- If a doctor has given you sodium, potassium or fluid limits, those override this.
- Batch B has no marine omega-3 (only plant ALA from walnuts). That's why Batch A has the fish.

# BATCH A: Mediterranean Sardine, Salmon & Tofu Quinoa Bowls (10 lunches)

Based on the evidence-checked Mediterranean recipe, adjusted to what's already in your kitchen.

## Ingredients

**Shared base (all 10 bowls)**

- 300 g dry white quinoa (Wellness Road, you have it)
- 2 × 420 g Woolworths Chickpeas No Added Salt, drained and rinsed
- 1 × 420 g Woolworths Black Beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 × 420 g Woolworths Red Kidney Beans No Added Salt, drained and rinsed
- 2 × 400 g diced tomatoes (check the label says 100% tomato, no added salt or sugar)
- 400 g sweet potato, peeled, 2 cm cubes
- 400 g carrots, thick half-moons
- 300 g red capsicum, chunky pieces (about 2 large)
- 300 g zucchini, thick half-moons (about 2 medium)
- 250 g brown onion (about 2 medium), divided
- 1 kg frozen broccoli and cauliflower (2 × 500 g bags)
- 280 g baby leaf spinach (1 bag)
- 60 mL oil in total (see method)
- 4-6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp ground cumin
- 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 0 to 1/2 tsp iodised salt, only after tasting

**Protein split**

- **4 sardine bowls:** 1 tin (110 g) sardines in springwater each
- **2 salmon bowls:** 1 small tin (95 g) pink salmon each
- **4 tofu bowls:** 450 g firm tofu in total, about 112 g per bowl

**At work, each bowl**

- 9 g walnuts, added after reheating
- 1 whole orange
- Optional: 250 mL calcium-fortified high-protein soy milk (So Good High Protein)

## Method

1. **Roast the veg.** Oven to 200 °C fan. Toss sweet potato, carrots, capsicum, zucchini and half the onion with 35 mL oil, 1 tbsp cumin, 1 tbsp smoked paprika and pepper. Spread over two trays, roast 30-35 minutes, turning once. The Bertolli light olive oil is fine for roasting.
2. **Roast the tofu.** Cut into 2 cm cubes, toss with 10 mL oil, a little paprika and pepper. Roast on a separate tray 20-25 minutes.
3. **Cook the quinoa.** Rinse well. Use 1 part quinoa to 2 parts water, covered, until the water is absorbed. Rest 5 minutes, fluff.
4. **Build the sauce.** In your biggest pot, soften the remaining onion and the garlic in 15 mL oil (use extra virgin here if you buy it). Add 1 tbsp cumin, 1 tbsp smoked paprika, oregano and pepper, stir for 1 minute. Add both tins of tomatoes, all drained beans and about 300 mL water. Simmer 10-15 minutes.
5. **Add the greens.** Stir in the frozen broccoli and cauliflower, cook 5-7 minutes until just tender.
6. **Fold together.** Off the heat, fold through the roasted veg, quinoa, spinach and lemon juice. Taste. Add salt only if it really needs it.
7. **Weigh and divide.** Put a large bowl on the scales, tare it, transfer the base in and note the total weight. Divide by 10. Weigh that amount into each container. The tofu goes in four containers, the sardines in four, the salmon in two (break the fish into chunks).
8. **Chill fast.** Use shallow containers, vent the lids briefly, refrigerate promptly, and freeze once cold. Never stack warm containers.

**Reheat** until steaming hot all the way through. Add walnuts and the orange after.

# BATCH B: Lentil, Edamame & Greens Brown Rice Bowls (10 lunches)

A fish-free, high-fibre, plant-protein batch with a warmer, spicier flavour than Batch A.

## Ingredients

**Base (all 10 bowls)**

- 350 g dry brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 1 kg frozen shelled edamame (2 × 500 g bags)
- 450 g firm tofu (calcium-set if possible)
- 300 g dry brown rice, rinsed (buckwheat groats are a good swap if you find them, and check they're gluten-free)
- 2 × 400 g diced tomatoes, no added salt (buy 2 more tins if you've used your stock)
- 600 g butternut pumpkin, peeled, 2 cm cubes
- 400 g carrots, half-moons
- 400 g cup mushrooms, halved
- 2 red onions, diced
- 1 bunch kale (about 200 g of leaves, stalks removed, chopped)
- 500 g frozen broccoli and cauliflower (1 bag)
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 20 g fresh ginger, grated
- 50 mL extra virgin olive oil in total
- 2 tbsp ground cumin, 1 tbsp ground coriander, 2 tsp ground turmeric, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp black pepper
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 0 to 1/2 tsp iodised salt, only after tasting
- No stock cube. Flavour comes from spices, ginger, garlic and lemon.

**At work, each bowl**

- 9 g walnuts
- 1 whole orange (the vitamin C helps you absorb the plant iron)
- About 5 mL extra virgin olive oil drizzled on after reheating (optional)

## Method

1. **Roast.** Oven to 200 °C fan. Toss pumpkin, carrots and mushrooms with 30 mL oil, 1 tbsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika and pepper. Roast 25-30 minutes. Roast the cubed tofu on a separate tray with 10 mL oil for 20-25 minutes.
2. **Rice.** Cook the brown rice with about 750 mL water, covered, for 30-35 minutes. Rest 5 minutes, fluff, and spread out to cool faster.
3. **Lentils.** Simmer in about 1.2 L water for 20-25 minutes until tender but still holding shape. Drain.
4. **Sauce.** Soften the red onions, garlic and ginger in 10 mL oil for 8 minutes. Add 1 tbsp cumin, the coriander, turmeric and pepper, stir 1 minute. Add the tomatoes and 300 mL water, simmer 10 minutes.
5. **Greens and edamame.** Add the kale and the frozen broccoli and cauliflower, cook 5 minutes. Add the frozen edamame, cook 3-4 minutes.
6. **Combine.** Off the heat, fold in the lentils, rice, roasted veg, tofu and lemon juice. Taste, season only if needed.
7. **Portion.** Weigh the whole batch, divide by 10, weigh into containers. Cool in shallow containers, refrigerate promptly, freeze once cold. Reheat until steaming hot.

## Rotating across 20 days

- Fish: 6 bowls (4 sardine, 2 salmon) from Batch A
- Tofu: 4 bowls in A, all 10 in B
- Legumes: every single bowl
- Calcium: from sardine bones, calcium-set tofu and the fortified soy. Spinach and kale don't count as calcium sources.
- Iron: plant iron in B is better absorbed with the orange or lemon alongside.
- Don't freeze the walnuts in the bowls, add them after reheating.

## Honest limits

- Vitamin D and B12 can't be confirmed from any of these, so don't rely on the bowls for them.
- The fibre load is very high. If 10 big bowls leave you uncomfortable, cut the grain next time, not the vegetables.
- Ten identical lunches is still the main weakness. If you tire of one, swap the protein or the veg and keep the pattern.
