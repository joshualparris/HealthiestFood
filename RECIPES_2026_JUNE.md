5. Recipe A - Salmon Brown Rice Tomato Veg Bowls
Makes 6 freezer lunches. Uses salmon, brown rice, tomato paste, baby
corn, peas and your fridge vegetables. Designed to be lemony,
tomato-rich and not dependent on salty stock.
Ingredient Exact amount for 6 meals Prep notes
Brown rice, cooked 900 g cooked rice Cook from about 300 g dry brown rice. Portion 150 g per container.
Canned pink salmon 3 x 415 g cans Drain lightly if very salty/watery. Mash soft bones in for calcium if
present.
Olive oil 30 mL About 2 tbsp.
Onion 200 g Dice finely. Use red/white/brown onion.
Carrot 350 g Small dice or grate for faster cooking.
Celery 250 g Dice.
Zucchini 200 g Dice. If your zucchini is smaller, use all of it.
Frozen peas 250 g Use straight from frozen.
Baby corn 1 x 410 g can Drain and chop.
Tomato paste 120 g Dilute into sauce.
Water 600 mL Mix with tomato paste.
Garlic powder 2 tsp / about 6 g Use instead of fresh garlic if needed.
Paprika 2 tsp / about 5 g Sweet/smoked both fine.
Ground cumin 1 tsp / about 2.5 g Optional but useful.
Dried herbs or dill 2 tsp dried or 10-20 g fresh Use what you have.
Black pepper 1/2 tsp / about 1 g Add to taste.
Lemon juice 45 mL About juice of 1 large lemon.
Stock powder 0-3 g max Optional. Skip if salmon/tomato paste is salty.
Method
1 Cook the brown rice first. Put 150 g cooked rice into each of 6 containers labelled A1-A6.
2 Heat 30 mL olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
3 Add onion, carrot and celery. Cook 8-10 minutes, stirring often, until softened.
4 Add zucchini, garlic powder, paprika, cumin, herbs and black pepper. Cook 2 minutes.
5 Mix 120 g tomato paste with 600 mL water. Add to the pot. Add stock powder only if using it.
6 Add peas and baby corn. Simmer 8-10 minutes until vegetables are cooked and sauce has thickened.
7 Add salmon gently. Break into chunks, but do not mash it into paste. Simmer 3-4 minutes.
8 Turn off heat and stir in 45 mL lemon juice. Taste. Add more lemon/pepper/herbs if needed, not extra salt.
9 Divide the salmon vegetable mixture evenly over the 6 rice containers. Target topping: about 360-450 g per
container. Final target: 510-600 g per lunch.
Health notes
• Good protein and omega-3 from salmon.
• Brown rice gives steadier carbohydrate than white rice for many people.
•
Lemon, paprika, herbs and pepper keep flavour high without leaning heavily on stock powder or soy sauce.
•
If sodium is a concern, drain salmon well and skip stock powder entirely.
6. Recipe B - Red Lentil Pumpkin Tofu Curry
Makes 6 freezer lunches. This is the highest-fibre batch. It uses red
lentils, tofu, chickpeas, pumpkin, peas, brown rice and curry spices. It
should freeze and reheat very well.
Ingredient Exact amount for 6 meals Prep notes
Brown rice, cooked 720 g cooked rice Cook from about 240 g dry brown rice. Portion 120 g per container.
Red split lentils 300 g dry Rinse until water is mostly clear.
Firm tofu 450 g Cube into 1.5-2 cm pieces.
Chickpeas 1 x 420 g can Drain and rinse if possible to reduce sodium.
Pumpkin 800 g Peel if needed; dice 1.5-2 cm.
Carrot 350 g Dice small.
Celery 250 g Dice.
Onion 200 g Dice finely.
Frozen peas 250 g Use straight from frozen.
Tomato paste 130 g Dilute into curry sauce.
Water 1,000 mL Start with this; add 100-200 mL if too thick.
Olive oil 30 mL About 2 tbsp.
Garlic powder 2 tsp / about 6 g Use instead of fresh garlic if needed.
Curry powder 2 tsp / about 5 g Use your preferred curry blend.
Turmeric 1 tsp / about 2.5 g Pairs with black pepper.
Ground cumin 2 tsp / about 5 g Adds depth.
Ground coriander 2 tsp / about 5 g Adds warmth.
Paprika 1 tsp / about 2.5 g Optional.
Black pepper 1/2 tsp / about 1 g Useful with turmeric.
Stock powder 0-5 g max Optional. Keep low for sodium control.
Lemon juice 30-45 mL Add at the end.
Method
1 Cook the brown rice first. Put 120 g cooked rice into each of 6 containers labelled B1-B6.
2 Rinse 300 g red lentils well. Set aside.
3 Heat 30 mL olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
4 Add onion, carrot and celery. Cook 8-10 minutes until softened.
5 Add garlic powder, curry powder, turmeric, cumin, coriander, paprika and black pepper. Stir for 1 minute.
6 Add pumpkin, rinsed lentils, drained chickpeas, tomato paste, water and stock powder if using. Stir well.
7 Simmer uncovered 25-30 minutes, stirring every few minutes so lentils do not catch on the bottom. Add a splash
more water if it becomes too thick before pumpkin softens.
8 Add tofu cubes and frozen peas. Simmer 8-10 minutes. Keep tofu pieces intact if possible.
9 Turn off heat and stir in lemon juice. Taste and adjust with more lemon, pepper or curry powder rather than salt.
10 Divide curry evenly over the 6 rice containers. Target curry: about 430-520 g per container. Final target: 550-650 g
per lunch.
Health notes
• This batch is the fibre anchor: lentils + chickpeas + vegetables.
• The tofu improves protein without adding much salt.
• Pumpkin and carrot add orange vegetables; peas add extra plant protein and fibre.
• Black pepper is included with turmeric, but the big health win here is the overall legume/veg pattern, not one spice
trick.
