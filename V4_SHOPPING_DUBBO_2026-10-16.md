### Actual consumption vs initial basket

- **Full pack purchases:** 3 × 500 g tofu = **1,500 g** all used, 4 × 500 g broccoli/cauliflower = **2 kg** all used, 3 × 210 g salmon = **498 g planned drained edible fish**, 8 × 110 g sardines = **600 g planned edible fish**, 4 × 400 g SPC tomatoes all used, 20 oranges all consumed if taken as sides.
- **Unused pantry carry-over:** ~200 g quinoa, ~700 g dry brown rice, ~150 g dried lentils, ~250 g peas, ~100 g ground flaxseed, ~30 g walnuts, ~415 mL EVOO, and most spice jars/salt. This is why the full initial basket divided by 20 overstates the marginal cost of repeating meals.
- **Savings:** check pantry for unopened suitable quinoa, rice, oil, walnuts, spices, lentils, tomatoes and soy; subtract *only confirmed usable packs* from the shopping bill, while recipe quantities stay unchanged.
- **Lettuce/cucumber/berries:** healthy options for fresh meals but **not necessary freezer ingredients**. Adding them blindly to the batch inflates price or degrades freezing texture. Seasonal fruit/vegetable rotation across weeks can improve variety.

## Safer substitutions, not silent ones

1. **Coles tofu out of stock:** [Woolworths Evergreen Fresh Hard Tofu 650 g](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/595465) lists calcium sulphate (516) and 24 g protein/100 g, but **no labelled calcium milligrams** and its Dubbo price/stock is unverified. Change the shopping weights (1,500 g tofu required); do not preserve this table's protein, calcium or cost figures. Another fallback is [Macro Firm 450 g](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/23038), 14.8 g protein/100 g but magnesium-chloride set — do not count it as a quantified calcium source. Use labelled fortified soy separately if needed.
2. **SPC tomato unavailable:** inspect other tins, choose genuinely **no added salt** (not the [salt-added Woolworths Italian diced tomatoes](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/218921)) and confirm allergy warnings.
3. **Black beans:** the ordinary [Woolworths 420 g black beans](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/907377) **contain salt** (150 mg sodium/100 g), so V4 specifically chooses [no-added-salt black beans](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/663581) (approx 8 mg sodium/100 g).
4. **Kale unavailable:** use **240 g baby spinach** in B (buy 2 more 120 g bags, adjust cost); do not treat spinach as a good absorbed-calcium source, and keep the soy drink/calcium-rich tofu approach.
5. **Fish cost too high:** choosing four fish lunches instead of eight can reduce cost, but then fish recommendations must be addressed through **other weekly meals**; don't continue reporting 2.75 fish serves/week from these lunches.
6. **Soy milk:** optional if sufficient daily calcium comes from other foods. For dairy-free consumers, check the exact high-protein *soy* milk label; avoid confusing it with products containing barley/malt or oat proteins if strict gluten-free requirements apply.
