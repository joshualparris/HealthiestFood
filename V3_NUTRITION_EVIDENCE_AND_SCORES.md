## What is actually supported by nutrition science?

1. **Australian dietary pattern:** vegetables, legumes, varied fruit, wholegrains, protein foods and healthy fats. [Eat for Health, dietary guidelines](https://www.eatforhealth.gov.au/guidelines/australian-dietary-guidelines-1-5).
2. **Marine omega-3 and fish:** Heart Foundation recommends **2–3 × 100 g cooked/tinned fish serves/week**, and ~250–500 mg marine EPA+DHA/day as population goal; plant ALA from walnuts is valuable but not identical. [Heart Foundation, June 2025](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/healthy-living/healthy-eating/omega-3-omega-6-heart-health). V3's labelled fish arithmetic ≈ **1,066 g drained / 4 weeks** but product omega-3 figures require actual nutrition panels; more fish is *not universally better*.
3. **Mediterranean-style eating pattern:** PREDIMED 2018 corrected reanalysis showed fewer major cardiovascular events among participants at elevated baseline cardiovascular risk allocated to Mediterranean diets with EVOO or nuts compared with the control arm. **This was not a trial of quinoa/sardine bowls, and cannot be extrapolated to exact lifespan gains.** [NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa1800389).
4. **Wholegrain and soy:** brown rice retains bran and is listed at **7.6 g protein, 3.9 g fibre/100 g uncooked** by SunRice; tofu **14.8 g protein, 6.2 g dietary fibre per 100 g** by Macro (confirm labelled values; unusual tofu fibre value may reflect formulation/testing). Neither proves a specific grain brand improves survival.
5. **Calcium/B12/D:** So Good High Protein **SOY** lists **10 g protein, ~400 mg calcium per 250 mL**, vitamins B12 and D, but also low amounts of added sugar and maltodextrin. Macro tofu is magnesium-chloride-set, **not automatically calcium-rich**. Dietary adequacy depends on *all* daily intake.
6. **Iodine:** FSANZ lists adult RDI 150 µg/day; **iodised salt in most bread** is mandatory but gluten-free diets may have fewer such foods. Source [FSANZ iodine and food](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/nutrition/iodine-sensitivities/iodinefood). No claim that one pinch of iodised salt guarantees sufficient iodine; avoid kelp or high-dose iodine for optimisation.
7. **Fruit, nuts and pepitas:** Green kiwifruit supplies vitamin C and fibre; walnuts provide ALA; unsalted pepitas supply nutrients like magnesium and zinc. These are modest **complementary** sources. **No RCT proves kiwi + pepitas extends lifespan more than orange + walnuts**, nor is topping every meal mandatory.
8. **Food safety:** NSW Food Authority: shallow portions, fridge/freezer within **2 hours**, cooked rice fridge **≤2 days**, freeze leftovers **≤3 months**, defrost in fridge/use within 24h, reheat till steaming hot and **never reheat rice twice**. https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers

## Label-based protein arithmetic — *not a full meal nutrient analysis*

- V3 B: Macro Firm Tofu **135 g raw/portion × 14.8% = ~19.98 g protein**; 100 g Australian baby peas × 4.8% = **4.8 g**; 30 g dry brown rice × 7.6% = **2.28 g**. **This subtotal ≈27.1 g protein before counting the 40 g dry lentils, mushrooms, tomatoes, greens, pepitas, walnuts or soy milk**; do not claim exact whole-lunch total without actual lentil/seed labels.
- With **250 mL high-protein soy** each lunch, add labelled **~10 g protein and ~400 mg calcium** if the purchased carton matches.
- V3 A: the salmon portions are **~83 g drained**, meaning they have less fish protein than the larger sardine portions; beans and quinoa contribute more, and soy drink may be helpful if trying to achieve a particular protein goal. Drained tin weights should be **measured at cooking**, not assumed exactly.

## Limits, price honesty and priorities

- **Prices** in the [V3 basket](V3_WOOLWORTHS_DUBBO_SHOPPING.md) mix indexed retailer offers, October 2026 price tracking and explicitly marked estimates. **We did not access a logged-in/live Dubbo Woolworths checkout or local inventory.** Product existence ≠ store stock.
- **No exact total kcal, fibre, sodium, omega-3, iron, calcium or vitamin D for full meals** can be certified without label-level spreadsheet calculation, actual drained/raw yields, topping options and food composition data. 20 identical-per-batch weights cannot eliminate individual nutrient needs.
- **Over-optimisation is a trade-off:** additions cost money/energy and raise preparation effort. The most effective upgrades are habitual variety, good overall diet, safety and convenience.
- **Recipe not kitchen-tested:** oven duration, final volume/texture, freezer texture, calorie needs and fish palatability require actual batch verification. Smaller bowls may be more appropriate depending on appetite/activity.

### Priorities before cooking
1. Verify gluten suitability and product stock/price for your Dubbo Woolworths store.
2. Confirm fridge/freezer can safely chill ten (not twenty) large portions.
3. Check the remaining pantry quantities before ordering.
4. After first cook, record actual weighed yield, whether 10 servings fit 750–1,000 mL containers, and whether the meals taste good enough to keep eating.
