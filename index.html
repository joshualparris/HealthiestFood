There is no scientifically proven “healthiest meal”.

These meals reflect food groups associated with healthy dietary patterns. A perfect 100/100 longevity rating would be invented. Individual nutritional needs, the rest of the diet and sustained habits matter.

01 / THE PATTERN

Plants, variety and fibre

Legumes, vegetables, fruit and wholegrains align with Australian dietary guidance. No single grain or organic label is a longevity guarantee.

Australian Dietary Guidelines ↗
02 / THE OMEGA FATS

Marine and plant sources

Oily fish provides marine EPA/DHA; walnuts and flax provide ALA. The Heart Foundation recommends oily fish 2–3 times a week within the overall diet.

Heart Foundation ↗
03 / CALCIUM CLARITY

Don't assume from the name

The hard tofu lists calcium sulphate but no calcium amount. So Good High Protein Soy is fortified; inspect the carton if you use it to support a dairy-free diet.

Check tofu label ↗
04 / FREEZER SAFETY

Cooling is part of cooking

NSW guidance: shallow portions, fridge/freezer within two hours, rice stored refrigerated for no more than two days, and reheat leftovers until steaming hot.

NSW Food Authority ↗
