# V2 consolidated Dubbo shopping and price sheet

**Research date: 16 October 2026. AUD. For all 20 lunches in [V2](RECIPES_V2.md).**

## What these prices mean

These are a transparent planning basket, **not a live Dubbo checkout or stock confirmation**. Public pages often hide Woolworths prices until a store is selected. I verified several product labels/listings and a small number of displayed prices; the remaining figures are explicitly estimates. Search-index captures may lag the shelf. Nothing has been ordered.

- **P:** retailer page displayed a price when researched, but location was not locked to Dubbo.
- **T:** third-party price tracker, with its date where available; not verified at checkout.
- **H:** historical amount recorded in the existing October recipe; not independently reverified.
- **E:** budgeting estimate, not an observed price. A precise sum of estimates is still an estimate.

Start with your pantry. The table deliberately assumes buying everything from scratch, so it does not silently rely on old stock. Its whole-pack total includes spare grains, oil and other ingredients. The allocated recipe cost charges fish/grain/oil approximately by amount used but conservatively charges whole spice jars and trim-heavy vegetables; it is not a precise consumed-food valuation.

## Specific label checks and substitutes

- **Tofu:** Pureland **High Protein Firm** 500 g is different from ordinary Pureland Tofu 500 g. The cheaper ordinary product was shown as reduced-to-clear/unavailable in one category result, so it is not used as a dependable price benchmark.
- **Fish:** compare sodium **per 100 g**, then look at drained yield, bones and price per edible amount. Salt-reduced sardines can be a good choice; plain sardines in olive oil are also reasonable but have a different energy/oil contribution. Do not discard usable fish solely to match a number: refrigerate/freeze surplus promptly for a separate meal.
- **Beans:** if a no-added-salt black-bean option is unavailable, use ordinary drained/rinsed beans or another no-added-salt legume. Do not promise rinsing removes a fixed percentage of sodium from every can.
- **Lentils:** choose 100% red split lentils, but check allergen statements. [Fresh Harvest 500 g](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/622037/fresh-harvest-red-split-lentils) lists possible milk/gluten/wheat cross-contact. For strict avoidance, buy a suitable alternative rather than assuming that listing is safe.
- **Vegetables:** broccoli/cauliflower without sauce; plain peas; any plain fresh/frozen greens. Frozen spinach is acceptable if cooked to its directions; buy an appropriate pack and use the same ingredient weight.
- **Tomatoes:** choose tomato/tomato juice with no added salt; citric acid is not a reason to reject an otherwise suitable tin. Avoid sweetened pasta sauce as an automatic substitute.
- **Oil:** plain extra-virgin olive oil. Organic certification is optional. Use the light olive oil you already own if necessary; switching oils is a modest refinement, not a reason to waste existing food.
- **Walnuts:** use 200 g total. If only the earlier 190 g pack is available, either buy extra or use **9.5 g per lunch** and label that change. Unsalted almonds are a practical alternative, but their omega-3 profile differs.
- **Milk:** 250 mL is a companion, not an ingredient to tip into the freezer containers. Choose at least **120 mg calcium/100 mL**; check dairy/gluten/allergen suitability and the individual product's fortification. Ordinary almond milk can have much less protein than the named high-protein product.

## Before paying

Check that the basket has **4 tomato cans, 3 × 500 g brassica bags, 1.5 kg usable tofu, 200 g walnuts, 160 mL worth of lemon juice and the required drained fish**, plus enough fruit and milk if using them. Adjust the prices here from your actual Dubbo checkout. Do not buy specialty supplements or superfood powders to make these meals healthy.
