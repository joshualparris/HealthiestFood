## Preserved source text

Got you — one big recipe that makes 10 separate freezer lunches.
Tomato lentil, sweet potato and vegetable rice bowls
Makes 10 adult lunch containers.
Ingredients
Rice
500 g uncooked white rice
Water according to packet directions
Lentil and vegetable mixture
2 tablespoons oil
2 onions, diced
4 garlic cloves, crushed
5 carrots, diced
3 celery stalks, sliced
1.2 kg sweet potato, peeled and cut into 2 cm cubes
2 zucchini, diced
500 g dry red lentils, rinsed
2 × 500 g jars tomato pasta sauce
1.8 litres water
2 cans corn, drained
6 tablespoons nutritional yeast
2 teaspoons salt
1 teaspoon black pepper
1 teaspoon turmeric, optional
Method
Cook the 500 g rice according to the packet. Once cooked, spread it across a tray or large dish so it cools quickly.

Heat the oil in your largest saucepan. Cook the onions, garlic, carrots and celery over medium heat for 7 minutes.

Add the sweet potato, zucchini, red lentils, tomato sauce, water, salt, pepper and turmeric.

Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat. Simmer uncovered for 30–35 minutes, stirring every five minutes so the lentils do not stick.

Check that the sweet potato and lentils are completely soft. Add another 250 mL water if the mixture becomes too thick before they finish cooking.

Stir through the drained corn and nutritional yeast. Cook for another 3 minutes.

Portioning
Into each of your 10 containers place:
About 150 g cooked rice, roughly ¾ cup
About 450–500 g lentil mixture, roughly 2 cups
Leave a small gap at the top because the food expands slightly when frozen.
Let the containers stop steaming, refrigerate them promptly, then freeze once cold. Label them with the date.
Reheating
Thaw overnight in the fridge, then microwave for 4 minutes, stir thoroughly, and microwave for another 1–2 minutes until steaming hot throughout.
Add avocado, cucumber, hummus or kimchi after reheating, rather than freezing them.

Each recipe makes 4 adult lunch portions and is dairy-free, provided the wraps and sauces are dairy-free.
1. Sweet potato and red-lentil tomato stew with rice
Ingredients
1 tablespoon oil
1 onion, diced
2 garlic cloves, crushed
500 g sweet potato, peeled and cut into 2 cm cubes
2 carrots, diced
200 g dry red lentils, rinsed
500 g tomato pasta sauce
750 mL water
1 teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon black pepper
300 g dry rice
Method
Cook the rice according to its packet.
Heat oil in a large saucepan. Cook onion and garlic for 4 minutes.
Add everything except the rice.
Bring to the boil, then simmer uncovered for 25–30 minutes, stirring every five minutes.
Add another 100 mL water if it becomes too thick.
Divide the rice and stew between four containers.
Reheat: Microwave from thawed for 3–4 minutes, stirring halfway.
2. Tuna, corn and vegetable pasta bake
Ingredients
300 g dry pasta
1 tablespoon oil
1 carrot, grated
1 zucchini, grated
185–200 g drained tuna
1 can corn, drained
500 g tomato pasta sauce
4 tablespoons nutritional yeast
½ teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon pepper
Method
Heat oven to 190°C fan-forced.
Cook pasta for two minutes less than the packet directions. Drain.
Fry carrot and zucchini in oil for 5 minutes.
Mix pasta, vegetables, tuna, corn, sauce, nutritional yeast, salt and pepper.
Spread in a baking dish and bake for 20 minutes.
Cool slightly and divide into four containers.
3. Salmon and sweet-potato patties with roasted vegetables
Ingredients
415 g canned pink salmon, drained
500 g sweet potato, peeled and chopped
250 g cooked rice
½ onion, very finely diced
4 tablespoons nutritional yeast
½ teaspoon pepper
¼ teaspoon salt
2 carrots, sliced
2 zucchini, sliced
1 tablespoon oil
Method
Heat oven to 200°C fan-forced.
Boil sweet potato for 15 minutes; drain and mash.
Mix salmon, sweet potato, rice, onion, nutritional yeast, salt and pepper.
Shape firmly into eight patties.
Place on a lined tray. Put carrot and zucchini beside them and drizzle vegetables with oil.
Bake for 25 minutes, carefully turning patties after 15 minutes.
Pack two patties and one-quarter of the vegetables into each container.
Best reheating: Thaw overnight, then microwave for 3 minutes or air-fry at 180°C for 7–8 minutes.
4. Vegetable and lentil bolognese
Ingredients
300 g dry pasta
1 tablespoon oil
1 onion, diced
2 celery stalks, finely diced
2 carrots, grated
1 zucchini, grated
200 g dry red lentils
500 g tomato pasta sauce
750 mL water
1 teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon pepper
3 tablespoons nutritional yeast
Method
Heat oil and cook onion, celery, carrot and zucchini for 7 minutes.
Add lentils, pasta sauce, water, salt and pepper.
Simmer uncovered for 25 minutes, stirring regularly.
Cook the pasta two minutes short of the packet time.
Mix pasta through the sauce and divide into four containers.
Sprinkle nutritional yeast through each portion before freezing.
5. Tuna and vegetable fried-rice bowls
Ingredients
300 g dry rice, cooked and cooled
1 tablespoon oil
1 onion, diced
2 carrots, finely diced
1 zucchini, finely diced
300 g cabbage, thinly sliced
1 can corn, drained
185–200 g tuna, drained
½ teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon pepper
Optional: 1–2 tablespoons soy sauce, if you have it
Method
Cook rice, spread it over a large plate and refrigerate until cold.
Heat oil in a large frying pan.
Cook onion and carrot for 5 minutes.
Add zucchini and cabbage; cook for another 6 minutes.
Add cold rice, corn, tuna, salt, pepper and optional soy sauce.
Stir-fry over high heat for 5 minutes.
Divide immediately into four shallow containers and refrigerate until cold before freezing.
6. Creamy cauliflower and red-lentil soup
Ingredients
1 tablespoon oil
1 onion, diced
2 garlic cloves
2 celery stalks, sliced
1 cauliflower, approximately 800 g, chopped
150 g dry red lentils
1.5 litres water
5 tablespoons nutritional yeast
1 teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon pepper
200 g dry rice, optional for a more filling lunch
Method
Cook onion, garlic and celery in oil for 5 minutes.
Add cauliflower, lentils, water, salt and pepper.
Simmer covered for 25–30 minutes.
Blend half the soup, leaving the remainder chunky.
Stir in nutritional yeast.
Serve with cooked rice mixed through, or freeze the soup by itself.
Pack: About 500–600 mL per container.
7. Lentil, rice and corn freezer burritos
Ingredients
8 wraps
150 g dry rice
150 g dry red lentils
1 onion, finely diced
1 carrot, grated
1 can corn, drained
200 g tomato pasta sauce
3 tablespoons nutritional yeast
½ teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon pepper
Method
Cook the rice.
Simmer lentils in 500 mL water for about 15 minutes; drain any excess.
Cook onion and carrot in a frying pan for 5 minutes.
Add rice, lentils, corn, sauce, nutritional yeast, salt and pepper.
Cook uncovered for another 5 minutes so the mixture is fairly dry.
Cool completely.
Place about ¾ cup filling through the centre of each wrap. Fold the sides inward and roll tightly.
Wrap each burrito separately, then freeze. Pack two per lunch.
Reheat: Remove wrapping and microwave from frozen for 3–4 minutes, turning halfway.
8. Creamy salmon and cauliflower pasta
Ingredients
300 g dry pasta
500 g cauliflower, chopped
2 garlic cloves
415 g canned salmon, drained
1 can corn, drained
5 tablespoons nutritional yeast
300 mL pasta cooking water
1 teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon pepper
Method
Cook cauliflower in boiling water for 12 minutes until very soft.
Remove cauliflower, then cook the pasta in the same water for two minutes less than directed.
Blend or thoroughly mash cauliflower with garlic, nutritional yeast, salt, pepper and 300 mL pasta water.
Mix the sauce through the pasta, salmon and corn.
Cook together over low heat for 3 minutes.
Divide into four containers.
Add 1–2 tablespoons of water before reheating, as the pasta will absorb sauce while frozen.
9. Beetroot, lentil and sweet-potato patties
Ingredients
1 can beetroot, thoroughly drained and finely chopped
150 g dry red lentils
300 g sweet potato, cooked and mashed
200 g cooked rice
½ onion, finely diced
5 tablespoons nutritional yeast
½ teaspoon pepper
¼ teaspoon salt
1 tablespoon oil
Method
Heat oven to 200°C fan-forced.
Simmer lentils in 500 mL water for 15 minutes, then drain well.
Squeeze excess moisture from the chopped beetroot using paper towel.
Mix everything except the oil.
Refrigerate mixture for 20 minutes.
Shape into eight firm patties and lightly brush with oil.
Bake for 30 minutes, turning carefully after 20 minutes.
Pack two patties per lunch with cooked rice or roasted vegetables.
These remain slightly soft, so reheating in an air fryer or oven gives the best texture.
10. Cabbage-roll-style lentil and rice bake
Ingredients
1 tablespoon oil
1 onion, diced
2 carrots, grated
400 g cabbage, thinly sliced
150 g white rice
150 g dry red lentils
500 g tomato pasta sauce
600 mL water
4 tablespoons nutritional yeast
1 teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon pepper
Method
Heat oven to 180°C fan-forced.
Cook onion and carrot in oil for 5 minutes.
Add cabbage and cook for another 6 minutes.
Transfer to a deep baking dish.
Stir through uncooked rice, rinsed lentils, sauce, water, salt and pepper.
Cover tightly with foil and bake for 40 minutes.
Stir, check that the rice is tender, then bake uncovered for another 10 minutes if needed.
Stir through nutritional yeast and divide into four containers.
Freezing safely
Let the steam subside, portion into containers, refrigerate promptly and freeze once cold. Label each container with the name and date and use within three months. Reheat until steaming hot throughout.
Keep avocado, cucumber, hummus, fresh dips and kimchi out of the frozen containers. Add them fresh after reheating. I also wouldn’t freeze the existing cooked leftovers unless you know exactly when they were cooked and how long they’ve been refrigerated.
