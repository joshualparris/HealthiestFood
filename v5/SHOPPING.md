# V5: specific Dubbo shopping list and honest costs

**Research date: 16 October 2026. AUD. Covers all four five-portion recipes.**

[Recipes](RECIPES.md) · [Evidence](EVIDENCE.md)

This is a **planning basket, not a confirmed Dubbo checkout**. Products are publicly listed at chains with Dubbo stores; local stock is unverified. Some search captures are weeks old, even when retrieved today. P = retailer displayed price; T = older third-party tracker; H = historical recipe price; E = estimated allowance. Never interpret the computed total as a verified till price.

## Where to shop

- **Coles Dubbo, Macquarie Street & Bultje Street:** the specific Pureland tofu, Tassal salmon, John West sardines and Wellness Road quinoa are listed by Coles. [Official store page](https://www.coles.com.au/find-stores/coles/nsw/dubbo-788).
- **Woolworths Delroy Park or Dubbo/Orana Mall:** practical sources for the Woolworths-brand staples. [Delroy Park location evidence](https://bws.com.au/storelocator/nsw-dubbo-west-1304), [Orana Mall location evidence](https://bws.com.au/storelocator/nsw-dubbo-6595), both described as attached to Woolworths.
- Select the actual Dubbo store in the supermarket app before ordering. A public product page does not establish local availability. No specific ALDI/IGA price or local independent availability was verified, so none is invented here.

A second shop is optional: buy equivalent plain products at one supermarket if convenient. Brand choice matters for the **nutrition label, sodium, calcium, allergens, price and taste**, not because a brand has been proved to maximise lifespan.

## Three sensible price choices

1. **Use the canned-salmon B variation:** budget two 415 g Woolworths Pink Salmon tins at an **estimated $7 each**, using 500 g drained total. Replaces the $50 frozen-salmon purchase with $14 and reduces core checkout to **$193.40**. It provides edible bones and is still a strong option, but sodium and energy differ. Net tin weight is not drained weight.
2. **Use Macro tofu if Pureland is unavailable or expensive:** four 450 g packs at the older tracked $2.80 total $11.20. Use 1.5 kg, leaving 300 g. Save **$6.80** versus the regular-price Pureland allowance, with less protein and unquantified calcium. This substitution invalidates the default meal estimates.
3. **Use what is already at home:** quinoa/rice, spices and oil do not need repurchasing. Subtract actual whole-pack purchases you avoid; do not subtract a fictional pantry balance.

At the captured Pureland $5.30 special, save $2.10 across three packs. At the captured Edgell black-bean $2 special, save $1 across two cans. Specials can end, and product/category pages sometimes disagree; regular-price budgeting avoids assuming a discount is available.

## Quantity check before cooking

The combined recipes need **900 g drained chickpeas, 500 g drained black beans, 1.2 kg tomatoes, 1.2 kg brassicas, 750 g carrots, 500 g capsicum, 500 g zucchini, 500 g peeled onion, 300 g spinach, 250 g peas, 300 g kale leaves, 500 g edible pumpkin, 300 g mushrooms, 1.5 kg tofu, 300 g dry quinoa, 300 g dry rice, 175 g dry lentils, 100 mL oil, 200 g walnuts, 160 mL lemon juice, 40 g garlic and 30 g ginger**, plus fish.

Four chickpea cans and two black-bean cans are purchase allowances based on expected yields. Weigh after draining; if short, either top up with another suitable can or record the smaller amount and distribute evenly. Do not use dry kidney beans without their separate safe soaking/boiling procedure.
