# V6 scientific audit, product-label evidence and V5 comparison

**Research: 16 October 2026.** Evidence hierarchy: Australian Heart Foundation, Australian Dietary Guidelines, NSW Food Authority, current Woolworths product labels, and dated third-party price captures. This is a **proposed** set of home-cooked recipes, not human trial evidence of extra lifespan, nor an independent nutrient laboratory analysis. [V6 recipes](RECIPES.md) · [shopping](SHOPPING.md) · [arithmetic script](verify.py).

## Is this the healthiest possible meal?

**Unknowable.** Even a good recipe cannot establish one universal "healthiest" lunch, because required energy and protein, the rest of the 24-hour diet, health conditions, gut tolerance, food allergies, preferences, and sustainable habits matter. Dietary guidance promotes variety across food groups; the Heart Foundation recommends a pattern including vegetables, legumes, wholegrains, nuts, unsaturated fats and oily fish. The PREDIMED diet trial evaluated a **dietary pattern** in people at cardiovascular risk—not these Woolworths bowls, not a perfect brand, and not individual lifespan extensions.

The measurable V6 improvements over V5 are **reliable availability of publicly listed Woolworths products**, **tighter editable weight and drained-weight arithmetic**, **separately measured fish and tofu**, **more explicit animal/plant protein trade-offs**, **clearer calcium ambiguity**, **separate five-portion cook-ups** and **stronger checkout-price provenance**. V5 already had the strongest food classes; V6 does **not prove** superior mortality or cardiac outcomes over V5, and new ingredients are not inherently healthier.

| Energy/satiety/tolerance | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | Individuals need different energy/fibre amounts |
| Cooking safety & repeatability | 9 | 8 | 9 | 9 | Five-portion batches; raw fish/rice require extra care |

**Do not chase a 100/100:** the rubric is deliberately conservative and not validated. Any tiny difference between A, B, C and D could simply be arbitrary scoring choice. The healthier pattern comes from rotating them and getting other nutrients across breakfast/dinner, not maximizing a single recipe score. No version of a recipe is "scientifically 100/100."

## Scientific literature and Australian guidelines

- [Heart Foundation, omega fats (updated June 2025)](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/healthy-living/healthy-eating/omega-3-omega-6-heart-health): **2–3 oily-fish meals/week**, including marine EPA/DHA; walnuts/flax ALA. Marine/plant omega-3 aren't interchangeable. Eating two to three fish lunches per week can fit guidance, but this isn't a requirement to exceed recommended whole-week consumption if dinners contain fish too.
- [Heart Foundation fish servings](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/blog/not-eating-enough-fish): **100 g tinned fish and 150 g fresh fish** as separate standard serves. **125 g raw salmon is a portion, not necessarily a full standard 150 g fresh-fish serve.** Therefore *fish lunch count* doesn't prove an exact 2–3 standard serves weekly; count whole-week actual cooked amounts. This corrects overconfident arithmetic in some prior documents.
- [Australian Dietary Guidelines](https://www.eatforhealth.gov.au/guidelines/australian-dietary-guidelines-1-5): physical needs, variety across food groups, minimise added salt/sugars/saturated fats. No requirement for a single meal to meet all micronutrients.
- [PREDIMED 2018 republished RCT, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa1800389): cardiovascular outcomes associated with Mediterranean diet *patterns* in high-risk participants; did not test these recipes, specific retailers or "life extension" claims.
- [NSW Food Authority leftovers](https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers): shallow rapid cooling; refrigerated/frozen **within two hours**, fridge <5°C, rice/pasta fridge **≤2 days**, other leftovers **≤3 days**, freezer up to **3 months**, reheating steaming hot, rice/pasta **once only**.
- [FSANZ food allergen labels](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/labelling/allergen-labelling): read current packaging. Natural gluten-free status is not a certified product guarantee.
- [Woolworths current product warning](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/595465): nutritional and allergen labels change. Select store in app to see local prices; no product page establishes Dubbo stock.

## Most useful real improvements after V6

1. **Do one five-portion kitchen test**: measure actual drained fish, cooked total, portion grams, taste, refrigerator capacity and reheating quality.
2. **Read actual labels** for gluten cross-contact, tofu protein, soy calcium and sardine sodium. Especially check the Evergreen tofu brand; fallback changes nutrient arithmetic.
3. **Check live Dubbo cart**: [SHOPPING.md](SHOPPING.md) flags every estimate; $256.43 is illustrative, not a Woolworths quote.
4. **Vary fruits, grains, legumes and proteins over weeks**, include calcium/B12/iodine/D at other meals as needed. Avoid piling more fibre/calories into a meal merely to chase theoretical nutrition points.
5. **Consider spending less:** canned salmon instead of Coho, use existing pantry quinoa/tomatoes/olive oil/spices, and count fish eaten at dinner. These are sensible swaps, not guaranteed health reductions.

**Score audit summary:** The numeric rubric above is an explanatory convenience with no validated ceiling, **not evidence that V6 beats V5 by a numerical margin**. Evidence is strongest for the long-term pattern and food safety, not the exact optimisation version number.
