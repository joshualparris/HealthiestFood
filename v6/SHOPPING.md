## Existing kitchen pantry: deduct after physically checking amounts

Photos from earlier conversation show some **Coles Wellness Road quinoa** (good single ingredient; use at least 300 g if available), at least two **Woolworths Essentials 400 g diced tomatoes** (read the label for salt/sugar), sweet potatoes, onions, Bertolli **light-tasting** olive oil, spices, cashews, a 1 L **So Good High Protein Soy** and another Vitasoy product. **Do not count pantry inventory as a real-time stock audit.** Regular olive oil is fine for roasting; it is not EVOO but also isn't unhealthy. Cashews are nutritious, just not a walnut ALA substitute. Two photographed Safcol sardine tins have a [Woolworths online 'may contain gluten/wheat/soy' warning](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/803409); check exact labels before using them in strict GF cooking. A photographed **Vitasoy Calci-Plus** lists barley/malt/gluten, so avoid in a gluten-free plan.

Fish-budget alternative: if fresh salmon is unavailable/too costly, substitute canned pink salmon with soft bones using **actual drained edible weight**. Recalculate cost, protein, omega-3 and sodium; do not claim that 210 g gross can gives 210 g edible. The aim is a realistic diet and delicious lunches, not expensive products for their own sake.

## Food allergy and safety check

Check label for fish, soy, wheat/gluten, peanuts/sesame, nuts and any cross-contact. **Do not assume all unprocessed foods are guaranteed gluten-free**. Soy milk: [So Good High Protein Soy](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/6021686) is labelled GF and calcium fortified; it **does contain cane sugar and maltodextrin**. Fish thawed from frozen should be held under 4°C and cooked within pack window; leftover cooked rice must be chilled quickly. See [NSW Food Authority leftovers](https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers).
