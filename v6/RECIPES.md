# V6 — Woolworths-only, four-batch freezer lunch rotation (Dubbo NSW)

**16 October 2026 | Proposed new recipe, not cooked or independently analysed | 20 adult lunches: four different batches of five | dairy-free; gluten-free only after checking every actual packet and the desired cross-contact threshold.**

**Navigation:** [exact Woolworths shopping, pack counts and price evidence](SHOPPING.md) · [nutrition evidence, V5 differences and audit](EVIDENCE.md) · [reproducible totals validation](verify.py). Older [V5](../v5/RECIPES.md) is preserved unchanged.

**Not a claim of scientifically perfect or “100/100” food.** Science favours a maintainable dietary pattern, not an arbitrary maximum of plants, fibre, protein, fish, supplements or calories. This is an actionable Woolworths-only alternative to V5's mixed Coles/Woolworths plan. Local stock and live Dubbo checkout prices were **not** confirmed. Every amount below refers to usable **raw edible** vegetable weight, dry grain/lentils, drained canned fish/beans, raw tofu and raw salmon **unless otherwise indicated**.

## Why V6 differs from V5

1. **One supermarket:** all named products have an identifiable Woolworths listing; V5's Coles-only Pureland high-protein tofu and Coles salmon are replaced. This makes shopping easier, **not automatically healthier**.
2. **Exact fish and tofu pack arithmetic:** 8 x 110 g John West sardines have roughly 8 × 75 g = **600 g drained**; 2 × 500 g Ocean Chef Coho packs supply five 125 g raw salmon portions (**625 g raw**) with **375 g of still-frozen raw salmon remaining**. Buy 2 x 650 g **Evergreen Fresh Hard Tofu**, using **625 g in each plant batch** and 50 g remaining.
3. **A strong Woolworths-labelled tofu:** Evergreen Fresh Hard has **24 g protein/100 g** and lists **calcium sulphate (516)** as a coagulant. However **its listing does not provide a calcium mg value**, so never promise a particular calcium amount from the tofu. The fortified-soy option is explicit.
4. **Stricter salt and allergen decisions:** no stock cubes or bottled pasta sauces, no added salt before tasting, no automatic substitution of photographed Safcol tins with 'may contain gluten/wheat' warnings; Woolworths chickpeas/black beans no added salt, washed/drained.
5. **No needless 'superfood arms race':** each lunch gets **10 g walnuts** after reheating, with **5 g ground flax only for the 10 plant bowls**. One whole fruit accompanies each bowl, alternating oranges and green kiwi. Those are sensible options, not demonstrated lifespan upgrades.
6. **Four separate five-lunch cook-ups** allow safer, faster cooling and real-world texture/taste feedback. Brown-rice instructions specifically protect against unsafe cooling/reheating.

## Four-week eating plan (20 work lunches)

| Week | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | C | B | D | A |
| 2 | C | B | D | A | C |
| 3 | B | D | A | C | B |
| 4 | D | A | C | B | D |

Each letter appears **five times**. Each week has **2–3 fish lunches** (A or B), while the remaining lunches contain tofu and legumes. Guideline quantities concern fish eaten over **the whole week**, including dinner. These 5 × 120 g drained sardine portions and 5 × 125 g **raw** salmon portions are **not** directly comparable to a tinned- or cooked-fish standard serve: salmon loses weight as it cooks.

### General technique and equipment

Use **five shallow, freezer-safe ~900–1,200 mL containers per batch** (sizes are initial suggestions, not a claim the untested cooked yield definitely fits), scales, sieve, oven trays, 2 pots, a food thermometer if available, labels/date marker. Chop vegetables to even sizes. Fish/soy/walnuts are allergens; follow current pack labels and prevent cross-contact. When frozen veg directions call for longer cooking than the times below, **follow the pack**. Chop and preheat while other steps are underway, but avoid holding cooked rice/beans warm on the bench.

### A — Lemon paprika sardines, chickpea and quinoa (5 servings)

**Buy/measure for THIS batch**
- **8 × 110 g John West Wild Caught Sardines in Springwater**; all sardines, **expected ~600 g drained** across five bowls. Combine fish and divide **actual drained mass by 5**; do not use gross tin weight.
- **2 × 420 g Woolworths No Added Salt Chickpeas**, drained/rinsed, **~504 g edible** total (target 500 g; simply use all from both tins).
- **150 g dry Macro Organic White Quinoa**.
- **1 × 400 g SPC Diced Tomatoes**, 100% tomato, liquid included.
- **400 g frozen Australian broccoli and cauliflower**, **250 g carrots**, **200 g red capsicum**, **200 g zucchini**, **100 g peeled brown onion**, **150 g baby spinach**.
- **25 mL extra virgin olive oil**, divided 15 mL roasting and 10 mL sauce; **10 g garlic**, **40 mL lemon juice** (measure), **2 level tsp cumin**, **2 level tsp smoked paprika**, **1 level tsp dried oregano**, **¼ tsp pepper**, **100–200 mL water** if needed. No added salt until tasting.
- **Serving only:** 10 g unsalted walnuts per meal (**50 g total**), 1 whole medium orange per meal (5 total). Optional 250 mL So Good High Protein **SOY** as a separate calcium-rich drink if it fits daily intake.

**Method: ~60–80 min plus cooling**
1. Heat oven to **190°C fan**. Cut carrots ~1 cm and capsicum/zucchini ~2 cm. Roast carrots, capsicum, zucchini in 15 mL EVOO with **1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp paprika** for **25–35 min**, turning once, until tender without blackened edges.
2. Rinse quinoa. Cook **150 g dry** according to **its own packet water ratio** (roughly 300 mL water but weigh/measure packet ratio), about 15 min; stand covered 5 min and fluff.
3. Cook 100 g onion in 10 mL EVOO for **6–8 min**. Stir in 10 g garlic and remaining spices 30 sec. Add 400 g tomatoes, chickpeas, 100 mL water; simmer **8–10 min**, adding more water to prevent drying.
4. Add frozen broccoli/cauliflower, return to simmer and cook **to packet directions**. Add 150 g spinach and wilt. Fold in roasted vegetables, quinoa and 40 mL lemon juice. Taste: fish will add salt, so usually none needed.
5. **Weigh the cooked shared base**, divide into **five equal weighed bases**. Open/drain/weigh all eight sardine tins together and divide fish across the five portions (**~120 g drained per bowl** if 600 g). Keep soft edible bones; mash if preferred.
6. Chill/freezer process below. Reheat until steaming hot throughout, add 10 g walnuts after, eat one orange.

### B — Lemon coho salmon, chickpea and green-brown-rice bowls (5)

**Ingredients**
- **625 g raw Ocean Chef Patagonian Coho Salmon Portions, Skinless** (five x **125 g raw**). Buy **2 × 500 g frozen packs**; keep the untouched, extra **375 g still frozen** for another meal following package instructions. **Only remove thaw-required portions**, never thaw the whole second pack just to break off one piece.
- **2 × 420 g No Added Salt Chickpeas**, drained/rinsed (~504 g edible total).
- **150 g dry SunRice Brown Medium Grain Rice**.
- **500 g frozen broccoli/cauliflower**, **250 g carrots**, **250 g frozen plain peas**, **150 g de-stemmed kale**, **100 g peeled brown onion**.
- **25 mL EVOO** (10 mL fish, 15 mL pot), **10 g minced garlic**, **40 mL lemon juice**, **1 tsp oregano**, **¼ tsp black pepper**, 100–200 mL water if needed.
- At serving: **10 g walnuts** and **one green kiwifruit** per meal (50 g walnuts, 5 kiwi total). Optional 250 mL fortified soy drink.

**Method: ~60–80 min plus safe cooling**
1. Thaw only the five required **125 g portions**, covered in the fridge; follow the product's label (**below 4°C and use within 24h of thawing; do not refreeze the uncooked fish**). Keep remaining frozen portions at ≤−18°C.
2. Heat oven to **180°C fan**. Rinse/cook **150 g dry brown rice** according to its pack, about 30–40 min. Spread cooked rice thinly for **rapid chilling** if other food will not be ready promptly; never leave a covered warm pot at room temperature.
3. Place five salmon portions on lined baking tray with 10 mL EVOO, oregano and pepper. Bake generally **12–20 min from thawed** depending on thickness; verify safe **~63°C at the thickest centre** using thermometer as a conservative fish-cooking target. Follow fish pack if stricter.
4. Sauté onion and carrot in 15 mL EVOO **8 min**, garlic for 30 sec; add drained chickpeas and 100 mL water. Add frozen broccoli/cauliflower + peas, bring back to simmer and cook for packet time, then add kale until soft.
5. Add lemon juice, divide rice and vegetable/chickpea mix **each into five equal portions**, then **one fully cooked salmon portion** to each. Label salmon as **125 g raw equivalent**, not 125 g cooked (moisture is lost).
6. Promptly chill/freeze; reheat thoroughly once. Add 10 g walnuts and a fresh kiwifruit after. **Canned-bone salmon alternate**: substitute fish using **actual drained edible weights**, but do not assume sodium/calcium/macros match fresh salmon.

### C — Ginger pumpkin red-lentil and hard-tofu curry (5)

**Ingredients**
- **625 g Evergreen Fresh Hard Tofu** (from first 650 g pack): five raw equivalents x **125 g**.
- **200 g dry red split lentils**, **150 g dry SunRice Brown Rice**, **1 × 400 g SPC Diced Tomatoes**.
- **500 g peeled/deseeded pumpkin**, **250 g carrots**, **300 g frozen broccoli/cauliflower**, **150 g baby spinach**, **150 g peeled brown onion**.
- **25 mL EVOO** (15 mL sauce, 10 mL finish), **10 g garlic**, **30 g fresh grated ginger**, **2 level tsp cumin**, **2 level tsp coriander**, **1 tsp turmeric**, **¼ tsp pepper**, **40 mL lemon juice**, **700 mL water initially** plus 200–400 mL optional as lentils swell.
- After serving: **10 g walnuts + 5 g ground flaxseed** per meal (50 g walnuts + 25 g flax total), one orange each (5). Fortified soy drink optional.

**Method: ~60–85 min plus cooling**
1. Rinse and cook **150 g dry brown rice** by its package; spread to cool promptly if waiting. Rinse **200 g red split lentils** until water runs clearer.
2. In a broad pot, soften onion/carrot with 15 mL EVOO for ~8 min; add grated ginger, garlic and spices for 30 seconds.
3. Add rinsed lentils, 500 g pumpkin, **700 mL water**, bring to boil, reduce to simmer, partly cover **15–25 min**, stirring regularly until pumpkin tender and red lentils fully softened; add measured water as necessary.
4. Add tomatoes and cubed **625 g tofu**, simmer gently **8–10 min**. Add frozen broccoli/cauliflower and cook fully to pack directions. Fold spinach in to wilt.
5. Stir in lemon and 10 mL remaining oil. Weigh/divide rice into five equal portions and curry into five equal portions; ensure **125 g raw tofu equivalent** per bowl by distributing tofu pieces evenly.
6. Chill/freeze promptly. On eating, thoroughly reheat and top with 10 g walnuts and 5 g ground flaxseed; have an orange.

### D — Smoky black beans, mushrooms, hard tofu and quinoa (5)

**Ingredients**
- **625 g Evergreen Fresh Hard Tofu** from second 650 g pack, five raw equivalents ×125 g; **2 × 420 g Woolworths Black Beans No Added Salt** (~504 g drained total), drained/rinsed.
- **150 g dry Macro Organic Quinoa**; **1 × 400 g SPC Diced Tomatoes**.
- **300 g mushrooms**, **300 g red capsicum**, **300 g zucchini**, **150 g de-stemmed kale**, **150 g peeled onion**.
- **25 mL EVOO** (15 mL sauté, 10 mL finish); **10 g garlic**, **2 tsp cumin**, **2 tsp smoked paprika**, **1 tsp oregano**, **¼ tsp pepper**, **40 mL lemon juice**, ~100–200 mL water if required.
- At serving: **10 g walnuts + 5 g freshly ground flaxseed**, **1 green kiwi** per meal (50 g walnuts, 25 g flax and 5 kiwi total). Optional 250 mL fortified soy drink.

**Method: ~45–65 min plus cooling**
1. Rinse and cook 150 g quinoa with **packet-directed** water; fluff when done.
2. Sauté onion in 15 mL olive oil 5 min; add mushrooms and cook another **6–8 min** until moisture has mostly evaporated. Add capsicum/zucchini and cook **5 min**.
3. Add garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano and pepper for 30 sec. Add tomatoes, drained black beans, cubed 625 g tofu, and ~100 mL water. Simmer **10–12 min**, stir gently without mashing tofu.
4. Add de-stemmed kale and simmer **4–6 min** until tender and completely hot. Finish with lemon and 10 mL oil.
5. Weigh and divide cooked quinoa equally across five containers; weigh/portion remaining tofu/bean/veg mixture equally, ensuring tofu distributed evenly. Cool/freeze promptly.
6. On eating, thoroughly reheat once, then add 10 g walnuts, 5 g flax and fresh kiwi.

## Food safety is essential, not optional

- Cook **one five-meal batch at a time** if cold-storage space is limited. Use separate shallow containers, air gaps for cooling, **refrigerate or freeze within two hours** of cooking. Never leave a giant pot at room temperature to cool completely.
- Fridge **below 5°C**, freezer **−18°C or colder**. NSW Food Authority says leftovers in fridge up to **3 days**, cooked rice/pasta **2 days**, frozen leftovers **up to 3 months**. Thaw in fridge and eat within 24 hours; microwave stir and reheat until steaming hot, **at least 60°C** per NSW consumer guidance. Rice and pasta should **never be reheated more than once**.
- A rice meal reheated for lunch must be eaten then or discarded, not refrigerated and microwaved again. Use an insulated cooler bag and ice brick on travel to work.
- Freeze the cooked salmon and fish bowl once (following safe thaw/cook/cooling), **not thawed raw salmon**. Keep fish refrigerated during prep and work surfaces clean. Avoid leaving prepared lunch in the danger zone 5–60°C.

## Dairy-free nutrient adequacy & allergy notes

**Optional calcium side:** [Sanitarium So Good High Protein Soy 1 L](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/6021686) has labelled ~10 g protein and ~400 mg calcium per **250 mL**, plus vitamins D/B12, and is described as gluten free. It **includes some cane sugar and maltodextrin**; not literally unsweetened. Five litres covers 20 ×250 mL if used with every lunch. It is not needed *with every lunch* if other meals provide sufficient calcium.

Evergreen Hard Tofu is **calcium-sulphate-set** and its label reports **24 g protein/100 g**, but calcium is **not stated numerically**. Don't calculate a calcium intake from the coagulant alone. Product may vary: check your actual packaging. Avoid [Vitasoy Calci-Plus](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/153587) if strictly avoiding gluten because its listed ingredients contain barley. John's sardines declare fish (not precautionary wheat on the captured label); previously photographed Safcol springwater tins carry online 'may contain gluten/wheat/soy' warning. Other substitutions must be checked pack-by-pack. Fish, soy, walnuts are allergens.

The meals needn't supply 100% of iodine, calcium, B12, vitamin D or any other nutrient; breakfast, dinner and snacks matter. Use iodised salt only to taste, **not extra salt to chase iodine**. Brown rice and lentils may have gluten cross-contact warnings even if naturally gluten-free. High fibre can be uncomfortable; start with smaller portions or reduce legumes/grains if needed. **The healthiest meal is one you'll consistently eat, safely and comfortably.**
