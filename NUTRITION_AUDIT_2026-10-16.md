## What we can and cannot score

There is no validated model that assigns a meal "100/100 longevity". The **10-metric, equal-weight rubric below is expert judgement**, not a clinical tool and not a scientific measurement of personal health. Scores are comparable *within this report only*; categories can overlap and are not necessarily equally important.

**Whole-rotation score:** not arithmetically the average of the bowls, since alternation helps match weekly fish targets. A subjective 90–94/100 would describe its strong alignment with an evidence-based eating pattern **but cannot be asserted as measured longevity benefit**. **We do not label anything 100/100 on health science.**

**100/100 = 10/10 on an explicitly narrower documentation and safety checklist** in the optimised recipe. That is the only honest 100 score possible from the evidence available.

## Ranked improvements by expected real-world value

1. **Diet-wide consistency and variety** (highest evidence): regularly eat vegetables, legumes, wholegrains, fruit and unsaturated fats, with fish/soy rotation; no magic single lunch. Meta-analyses and Heart Foundation support *patterns*, not precise ingredient/perfect-brand claims.
2. **Fish edible weights and weekly distribution:** Original "4 sardine + 2 salmon" lunches provide only ~300 g sardines (4 × 75 g drained) plus ~166 g from a 210 g salmon tin (or two small tins of uncertain drained yield); across four five-day weeks this is ~117 g/week, not 2–3 x 100 g servings. New A: **900 + 166 = ~1,066 g / four weeks = ~267 g/week**. If fish meals at dinner are already being eaten, keep lunch protein more varied; fish targets concern whole-week diet.
3. **GF dairy-free label correctness:** Vitasoy Calci-Plus **contains barley/malt and is marked gluten**; So Good High Protein *soy* says gluten-free (not So Good Protein *oat*). Safcol sardines carry an online cross-contact warning. Macro tofu is magnesium-chloride set and its calcium content isn't established by its protein figure.
4. **Stop treating pods as edible edamame:** Woolworths "Seapoint 340 g Shelled Edamame" title conflicts with its product description "soybeans in pods"; another "Umami whole edamame 1 kg" lists 1% salt and 410 mg sodium per 100 g. Replaced with 1 kg plain frozen peas plus 1.35 kg firm tofu. Reassess if you obtain a genuinely shelled, low-sodium product.
5. **Energy/protein flexibility:** With changed fish/tofu/soy servings, **avoid retaining old 500–650 kcal/28–35 g protein/16–20 g fibre as precise**, since previous figures were rough. Labs aren't necessary for healthy eating but exact macronutrient claims require full weighed-drained recipe and label-level Australian food composition calculations. High fibre may cause bloating; no evidence that forcing maximal fibre or minimal energy extends life.
6. **Freezer-safe preparation, especially rice:** small shallow containers, fridge or freezer within 2 hours, rice refrigerated ≤2 days, leftovers frozen ≤3 months and steaming-hot reheat once. Cooking 20 meals at once may exceed fridge cooling capacity.
7. **Don't chase speculative superfoods:** Organic quinoa, premium EVOO brands, exotic powders, special spices, supplements and polyphenol hype lack evidence of being uniquely essential. An affordable habitual good diet beats an expensive short-lived project.

## Strong evidence / limits (primary sources)

- **Heart Foundation 2025 omega-3 guidance**: 2–3 x 100 g cooked/tinned fish serves/week; ~250–500 mg/day marine EPA/DHA goal; walnuts for plant ALA. https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/healthy-living/healthy-eating/omega-3-omega-6-heart-health
- **Heart Foundation heart-healthy eating pattern**: veg, legumes, wholegrains, fats/oils, nuts, fish, reduce excess salt. https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/healthy-living/healthy-eating/heart-healthy-eating-pattern
- **PREDIMED (NEJM 2018 reanalysis after randomisation irregularities)**: adults 55–80 at high cardiovascular risk had lower major cardiovascular event incidence with Mediterranean-style diets supplemented by EVOO or nuts versus dietary-fat reduction advice; it **did not test these recipes** and does not prove longevity for everyone. https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa1800389
- **NSW Food Authority leftovers**: fridge/freezer within 2h, cooked rice/pasta fridge ≤2d, frozen leftovers ≤3mo, reheat once for rice. https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers
- **FSANZ allergen rules**: gluten-containing barley/wheat declared; "may contain" is voluntary precautionary labelling and requires individual risk assessment. https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/labelling/allergen-labelling
- **SPC 100% tomatoes** no added salt/sugar label: https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/322960
- **210g salmon** nutrition panel two 83g portions, 317 mg sodium / serving, fish bones: https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/220696
- **110g sardines** 75g drained labelled serve, 176 mg sodium / serve: https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/98523

## Necessary qualifications

- Real Dubbo Woolworths prices/stock were **not checkout-verified**; see shopping markdown's price provenance and estimates.
- No recommendation that calcium-fortified milk must be consumed at every lunch; its usefulness depends on the remainder of the dairy-free diet.
- Vitamin D, iodine, iron, B12 and calcium adequacy across 24 hours aren't guaranteed by one meal. Medical restrictions, allergies, energy demands and gut tolerance can change the best choice.
- Recipe and ingredient changes are **new proposed cooking instructions** and have not yet been physically made, tasted, tested for reheating texture, or independently nutrient-analysed.
