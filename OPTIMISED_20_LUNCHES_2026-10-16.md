## Four-week schedule

Alternate A (fish) and B (plant-based) across 20 weekdays. **Week 1 A/B/A/B/A; week 2 B/A/B/A/B; repeat those two weeks.** This produces **10 fish lunches over 4 weeks** (2–3 each week), alongside 10 tofu/lentil lunches.

- Eight sardine portions: 12 × 110 g John West springwater sardines, **75 g drained per tin**, mixed and distributed equally as **8 × ~112.5 g drained fish**.
- Two salmon portions: 1 × 210 g Woolworths pink salmon with bones, **2 × 83 g drained** (rather than 210 g edible).
- Total planned fish **~1,066 g drained / four weeks = ~267 g/week = 2.7 × 100 g standard tinned fish serves/week**. Actual fish weight must be checked after draining; the two 83 g salmon portions are not 100 g each. The fish amounts don't guarantee marine omega-3 targets in isolation. Heart Foundation: https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/blog/not-eating-enough-fish

### A — Mediterranean sardine/salmon, legume and quinoa bowls (10)

**Shared base, before fish**: 300 g *dry* plain quinoa; 2 × 420 g Woolworths No Added Salt chickpeas (drain/rinse); 1 × 420 g Woolworths black beans (drain/rinse); 1 × 420 g Woolworths No Added Salt kidney beans (drain/rinse); 2 × 400 g SPC diced tomatoes (100% tomato); 400 g peeled sweet potato; 400 g carrots; 300 g red capsicum; 300 g zucchini; 250 g brown onion; 2 × 500 g Woolworths Frozen Australian Broccoli & Cauliflower (1 kg); 280 g baby spinach; 4–6 garlic cloves; juice of 2 lemons; 60 mL olive oil (extra virgin preferred, existing regular olive oil fine); 2 tbsp ground cumin; 2 tbsp smoked paprika; 2 tsp dried oregano; 1 tsp black pepper; iodised salt **up to ½ tsp for all ten bowls only after tasting**.

**Protein added after dividing base:** A1–A8 each get ~112.5 g drained sardines (12 tins distributed equally); A9 and A10 each receive ~83 g drained pink salmon (1 × 210 g tin). Keep edible soft bones. **No tofu in A**; protein comes from fish, beans and quinoa.

**Each lunch at serving:** 9 g walnuts added after reheating, 1 medium orange, optionally 250 mL calcium-fortified So Good High Protein Soy milk as part of daily calcium intake.

**Cooking:**
1. At 200°C fan, roast 400 g sweet potato, 400 g carrots, 300 g capsicum, 300 g zucchini and half the onion on uncrowded trays with 35 mL oil, 1 tbsp cumin/paprika, pepper, ~30–40 min.
2. Rinse and cook 300 g dry quinoa to package directions (typically ~600 mL water); fluff.
3. In a large pan cook remaining onion and garlic in 15 mL oil. Add remaining 1 tbsp cumin, 1 tbsp paprika, oregano; stir 1 min. Add all four drained/rinsed bean tins, the two tomato tins, ~300 mL water; simmer 10–15 min.
4. Cook frozen broccoli/cauliflower completely to packet directions. Combine vegetables, cooked quinoa, spinach, sauce and lemon; reserve the final 10 mL oil for finishing. Taste before adding salt.
5. **Weigh all the shared cooked base and divide by 10.** Drain fish, combine the 12 sardine tins, weigh and divide their drained contents into eight equal piles; separately weigh/split drained 210 g salmon tin into two. Add fish to the 10 bases. Portion into shallow containers.
6. Cool safely (see below), refrigerate promptly and freeze. Reheat one portion until steaming hot, stirring midway; finish with 9 g walnuts, eat orange.

### B — Lentil, pea, tofu, kale and brown-rice bowls (10)

**Why peas replace original shelled edamame:** Woolworths search listings for "shelled edamame 340 g" have contradictory "in pods" descriptions; other 1 kg listings are *salted whole pods*. Counting a 1 kg pack of pods as 1 kg edible beans falsely inflates the estimated protein. We therefore specify actual verified **shell-free frozen peas** and 3 packs of high-protein tofu. **Optional edamame swap:** use 1 kg *edible shelled*, low-salt edamame only if packet verified; then recalculate nutrients/pricing.

**Shared ingredients:** 400 g *dry* brown/green lentils (NOT red split); 300 g *dry* brown wholegrain rice; 1 × **1 kg Woolworths Australian Baby Peas** (plain frozen peas); **3 × 450 g Macro Firm Tofu = 1,350 g** (~135 g per bowl); 2 × 400 g SPC plain diced tomatoes; 600 g **peeled** butternut pumpkin; 400 g carrots; 400 g mushrooms; 2 medium red onions (~250 g peeled); 200 g de-stemmed kale leaves (buy enough bunches to yield this); 1 × 500 g frozen broccoli/cauliflower; 6 garlic cloves; 20 g peeled fresh ginger; juice of 2 lemons; **50 mL EVOO** (30 mL roasting veg, 10 mL roasting tofu, 10 mL sauce); 2 tbsp cumin; 1 tbsp coriander powder; 2 tsp turmeric; 1 tsp smoked paprika; 1 tsp pepper; optional iodised salt max ½ tsp for whole batch.

**Each lunch at serving:** 9 g walnuts; 1 orange; optional 250 mL So Good High Protein Soy milk.

**Cooking:**
1. Roast pumpkin, carrots and mushrooms in 30 mL EVOO with 1 tbsp cumin and paprika at 200°C fan, 25–35 min. Roast tofu (all 1,350 g) on separate uncrowded trays with 10 mL oil, 20–25 min.
2. Rinse/cook 300 g dry brown rice to package directions; spread in shallow dishes for quick cooling. Simmer 400 g brown/green lentils in plentiful water 20–30 min until tender, drain.
3. Sauté onions, ginger and garlic in 10 mL EVOO; add remaining spices and tomatoes, ~300 mL water; simmer ~10 min.
4. Cook **all** frozen peas and frozen broccoli/cauliflower through following packets (not just warm them briefly); simmer kale into tomato sauce.
5. Fold sauce, lentils, rice, vegetables, tofu and lemon together, taste, divide into ten **equal-weight** shallow meal containers.
6. Promptly chill and freeze; reheat once, thoroughly; finish with walnuts and orange.

## Allergens, calcium, diet reality
- **Vitasoy Calci-Plus contains barley flour, barley malt and gluten:** don't use for a gluten-free meal. Source: https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/153587
- The photographed **Safcol Brisling Sardines** carry an online 'may contain gluten, wheat, soy' precautionary warning; avoid as an automatic substitution if strict gluten avoidance is required. Source: https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/803409
- **So Good High Protein Soy 1 L** explicitly states gluten-free and roughly **10 g protein, 400 mg calcium per 250 mL**; check your own label. It contains some sugar and maltodextrin, so is not literally unsweetened. Source: https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/6021686
- Macro Firm Tofu contains soy, has ~14.8 g protein per 100 g and uses **magnesium chloride**, so **do not count on a quantified calcium contribution**; fortified soy drink and fish bones are reliable alternatives.
- As standalone meals these need not cover every daily vitamin/mineral. **Iodine, B12, vitamin D and dietary adequacy** depend on foods outside lunch, your actual fortified drinks, and requirements; don't indiscriminately supplement or add excess salt.

## Cooling/freezing and reheating
Cook the two ten-serving batches on **separate days** if fridge/freezer cooling capacity is limited. Spread in shallow containers and cool rapidly: put in fridge or freezer **within 2 hours** of cooking. Fridge <5°C and freezer at -18°C; NSW Food Authority recommends leftovers refrigerated up to 3 days, cooked rice/pasta up to 2 days, and frozen leftovers for up to **3 months**. Defrost in fridge, use within 24 hours and reheat to steaming hot throughout. **Never reheat cooked rice more than once.** Source: https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers

## Honest nutrition notes
