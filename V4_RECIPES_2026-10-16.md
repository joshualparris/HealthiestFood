# V4 — Dubbo's practical evidence-led 20 freezer lunches

**Version:** 4 | **16 October 2026** | **Status:** proposed, not cooked or taste-tested | **Serves:** 20 adult work lunches in two separately cooked batches of 10 | **Diet:** dairy-free, with gluten-free ingredients **only if the packages you actually buy meet that requirement**.

**What's new versus V3:** 8 correctly weighed oily-fish lunches (four sardine, four salmon) plus **12 tofu/legume lunches** (two in A and ten in B); a specifically calcium-labelled, high-protein tofu; explicit *no-added-salt* black beans; deliberately moderate flax/walnut additions rather than a race to maximum fibre; portions based on edible/drained fish weights; a shop split between Woolworths and Coles Dubbo and an audited price table. More variety and better nutrient certainty, **not proven more years of life**.

Links: [V4 exact shopping & prices](V4_SHOPPING_DUBBO_2026-10-16.md) · [V4 evidence, calculations & substitutions](V4_EVIDENCE_AUDIT_2026-10-16.md) · [V3 preserved unchanged](OPTIMISED_20_LUNCHES_2026-10-16.md).

## Shopping decisions that matter

- **Fish:** [John West sardines in springwater 110 g](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/98523) lists **75 g drained edible fish per tin**, not 110 g. [Woolworths Pink Salmon 210 g](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/220696) lists **2 × 83 g edible fish per tin** and contains soft edible bones. Weigh after draining and redistribute equally.
- **Tofu:** [Coles Pureland High Protein Firm Tofu 500 g](https://www.coles.com.au/product/pureland-high-protein-firm-tofu-500g-6848120) lists **17.9 g protein and 120 mg calcium per 100 g**. Buy **three packs = 1,500 g**: two A portions ×125 g and ten B portions ×125 g. This provides a predictable calcium contribution from tofu, unlike the magnesium-chloride-set Macro Firm 450 g used in V3. Available on Coles' national product site; **Dubbo shelf stock remains unverified**.
- **Tomatoes:** [SPC diced tomatoes 400 g](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/322960) contain 100% tomatoes with no added sugar/salt. Don't silently substitute the salt-added Woolworths Italian diced tomatoes.
- **Beans:** choose explicitly labelled [chickpeas no added salt](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/259399), [black beans no added salt](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/663581) and [kidney beans no added salt](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/259401). Drain, rinse and weigh the edible portions.
- **Drink:** optional 250 mL [So Good High Protein Soy](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/6021686) with lunch or at another meal supplies 10 g protein and labelled calcium fortification, subject to the carton label. It is *not* sugar-free, and is not required if overall calcium intake is already adequate.
- **Fresh sides kept separate:** 8 g walnuts, 5 g ground flaxseed and 1 orange after reheating. This keeps the nuts crunchy, prevents unnecessary heavy oil/seed load inside frozen meals, and supplies fresh vitamin C with pulses.

# Batch A — Mediterranean three-protein quinoa, legumes & greens

**Yield:** 10 large containers: 4 sardine, 4 salmon, 2 tofu. **Elapsed cook/prep:** approximately 80–110 minutes before chilling; allow extra time with one oven or small trays.

### Shared base for ALL 10 containers

| Ingredient | Precise recipe quantity |
|---|---:|
| Plain dry quinoa | 300 g |
| No-added-salt chickpeas | 2 × 420 g cans, drained and rinsed |
| No-added-salt black beans | 1 × 420 g can, drained and rinsed |
| No-added-salt kidney beans | 1 × 420 g can, drained and rinsed |
| SPC no-added-salt diced tomatoes | 2 × 400 g cans |
| Sweet potato | 400 g **peeled** cubes |
| Carrot | 400 g, sliced |
| Red capsicum | 350 g **edible** strips |
| Zucchini | 350 g, thick half-moons |
| Brown onion | 250 g peeled, chopped; divide in half |
| Frozen Australian broccoli & cauliflower | 2 × 500 g bags = 1,000 g |
| Baby spinach | 2 × 120 g bags = 240 g |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | **40 mL total**: 25 mL roast + 15 mL sauté |
| Garlic | 5 cloves, finely chopped |
| Lemons | juice of 2, added at finish |
| Cumin, smoked paprika, dried oregano | 2 tsp of each |
| Black pepper | ½ tsp, then adjust |
| Water for sauce | 300–450 mL, add gradually |
| Iodised salt | Optional, **no more than ½ tsp for the entire A batch**, only after tasting; most people need less |

### Protein allocations — add to individual containers, not to the communal base

| Label | Number | What goes into EACH container |
|---|---:|---|
| **A-SARD** | 4 | **2 × 110 g sardine tins**, drained: ~150 g *edible fish* (8 tins total) |
| **A-SALM** | 4 | **~124.5 g drained salmon**, from 3 × 210 g tins = ~498 g drained total |
| **A-TOFU** | 2 | **125 g Pureland High Protein Firm Tofu**, cooked first (250 g total) |

If weights on your kitchen scales differ from label estimates, divide the **actual combined drained sardines by 4** and **actual combined drained salmon by 4**; do not throw away edible soft bones. For the tofu bowls, include all 125 g, and check it is hot throughout after roasting.

### Cooking method — make in this order

1. **Set out ten clean, shallow, freezer-safe containers** (roughly 750–1,000 mL capacity); label 4 A-SARD, 4 A-SALM and 2 A-TOFU. Ensure fridge/freezer has room; otherwise make **two half-batches**.
2. **Oven:** heat to **200°C fan-forced**. Wash and chop sweet potato (400 g edible), carrots (400 g), capsicum (350 g), zucchini (350 g) and **125 g** of the onion. Toss with **25 mL EVOO**, 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp smoked paprika. Spread across uncrowded trays, roast **30–40 minutes**, tossing halfway, until the sweet potato is tender.
3. **Quinoa:** wash **300 g dry quinoa** in a sieve, then cook with about **600 mL water** or the exact packet ratio. Bring to boil, cover, simmer **13–16 minutes**; rest off heat 5 minutes. Check that all grains are cooked. Keep hot until portioning or quickly chill if waiting.
4. **Tomato/legume base:** sauté remaining **125 g onion** and 5 garlic cloves in **15 mL EVOO** for 5–7 min. Stir in remaining cumin/paprika and **2 tsp oregano** for 30 seconds. Add **800 g tomato**, four drained/rinsed bean tins and **300 mL water**; simmer **12–15 minutes**, adding only enough of the remaining 150 mL water to keep it spoonable.
5. **Crucifers:** cook the full **1 kg frozen broccoli/cauliflower** until hot and tender **following the pack directions** (allow ~6–9 minutes after the liquid returns to simmer). No raw frozen vegetables should be merely tossed into a cooling pot.
6. **A-tofu:** cube **250 g Pureland tofu**, roast separately on a non-stick tray **15–20 minutes** until hot throughout. No extra oil required; turning once helps browning. Keep separate from fish.
7. **Combine shared base:** fold hot quinoa, roasted vegetables, broccoli/cauliflower, spinach and lemon juice through the hot tomato/bean mixture. Spinach should wilt. Taste; add pepper, lemon or herbs before any extra salt.
8. **Equal base first:** weigh the combined cooked base (subtract pot weight), divide its weight by **10**, and put precisely that amount in each container. This is important because fish/tofu portions are different sizes.
9. **Add proteins:** open all eight sardine tins and **three salmon tins**; drain and weigh separately (the can's gross weight is not the food weight). Divide sardines equally across four A-SARD; divide salmon equally across four A-SALM. Add **125 g cooked tofu** to each A-TOFU. Mix each portion gently with its own spoon.
10. **Chill promptly:** place uncovered/loosely vented shallow portions into fridge with air space; **do not wait for the whole batch to cool on the bench**. Seal when cool and freeze promptly at **−18°C or colder**. Record cook date, protein and weight on each label.

### When eating Batch A

Thaw a container overnight **in the fridge** (or microwave-defrost safely). Microwave in intervals, stir right through to the middle and continue until steaming hot. Add **8 g walnuts** and **5 g freshly milled flaxseed** only after reheating; have **1 medium orange** alongside. Optional **250 mL calcium-fortified soy milk** if useful toward your overall daily calcium/protein intake. The orange, nuts, flax and soy drink are **not frozen inside the bowl**.

# Batch B — Tomato-ginger lentil, tofu, kale & roasted vegetable brown-rice bowls

**Yield:** 10 plant-based containers. **Elapsed prep/cook:** approximately 85–110 minutes before chilling. The recipe uses the remaining **1,250 g Pureland tofu** in exactly ten 125 g portions.

### Ingredients for all 10

| Ingredient | Precise recipe quantity |
|---|---:|
| Dry brown/green lentils (**not red split**) | 350 g |
| Dry wholegrain brown rice | 300 g |
| Pureland High Protein Firm Tofu | **1,250 g** (10 × 125 g; remainder of the 3 bought packs) |
| Plain frozen green peas (out of pod) | **750 g** (keep remaining 250 g frozen) |
| Butternut pumpkin | **900 g peeled/cubed**; buy ~1.2 kg unpeeled |
| Carrots | 400 g |
| Mushrooms | 500 g |
| Red onion | 250 g peeled |
| Kale | **200 g leaves** with thick stalks removed (buy enough bunches to reach this; wash well) |
| Frozen broccoli/cauliflower | 2 × 500 g = **1 kg** |
| SPC no-added-salt diced tomatoes | 2 × 400 g |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | **45 mL**: 25 mL veg + 10 mL tofu + 10 mL sauce |
| Fresh garlic | 6 cloves |
| Fresh ginger | 20 g peeled/grated |
| Lemons | Juice of 2 |
| Cumin | 2 tsp |
| Ground coriander | 2 tsp |
| Turmeric | 1 tsp |
| Smoked paprika | 1 tsp |
| Ground pepper | ½ tsp |
| Water for sauce | ~300 mL, add as required |
| Iodised salt | Optional max ½ tsp across ten portions, after tasting |

### Cooking method — no complicated simultaneous juggling

1. **Put ten shallow freezer containers out**, labelled B1–B10. Ensure adequate fridge/freezer capacity first. Set oven to **200°C fan**.
2. **Roast 900 g peeled pumpkin + 400 g carrots + 500 g mushrooms** with **25 mL EVOO**, 1 tsp cumin and smoked paprika, spread over uncrowded trays. Roast **30–40 minutes**, turning once, until pumpkin/carrot are tender.
3. **Roast 1,250 g cubed tofu** separately using **10 mL EVOO** across the tofu trays, about **20–25 minutes**, turning once. Verify centre is hot; cut into ten **125 g** weighed sets after cooking only if the cooked yield remains close, otherwise divide actual cooked tofu into ten equal portions.
4. **Rice:** rinse and cook **300 g dry brown rice** according to its packet (roughly 30–40 minutes for many kinds, with the packet's water ratio). Keep hot until portioning; if ready early, spread in shallow trays and promptly refrigerate rather than holding warm.
5. **Lentils:** rinse **350 g dry green/brown lentils**, cover with plenty of fresh water (around **1.2–1.5 L**) and simmer **20–30 minutes**, until tender but holding their shape. Drain. If buying canned lentils as a substitute, use a genuinely equivalent **drained cooked weight** and adjust cooking/sodium claims; do not treat 350 g canned as 350 g dried.
6. **Tomato-ginger sauce:** cook **250 g red onion**, 6 garlic cloves and **20 g ginger** in **10 mL EVOO** for 6–8 minutes. Stir in cumin, coriander, turmeric and pepper for 30 seconds; add **800 g diced tomatoes** and ~300 mL water, simmer 10 minutes.
7. **Cook the vegetables:** add **1 kg frozen broccoli/cauliflower** and **750 g peas** to hot sauce in manageable portions and simmer until both are cooked through **to packet instructions**. Add **200 g finely chopped kale leaves** for the final 4–5 minutes until soft; remove tough stems.
8. **Combine:** fold in cooked lentils, cooked rice, roasted veg, cooked tofu and juice of two lemons. Taste and use more ginger, citrus or herbs rather than salt. The vegetables and lentils must be fully cooked, not just warmed through.
9. **Weigh total finished edible batch**, subtract pot weight, divide into exactly ten equal finished-container portions. If tofu was portioned separately, distribute sauce/grains/vegetables equally and add **one tenth of the cooked tofu** to each.
10. **Chill/freezer workflow** as per A: shallow portions, prompt fridge cooling, then freeze at −18°C. Never keep a giant pot of hot food on the bench waiting for it to cool naturally.

### When eating Batch B

Reheat thoroughly, stirring midway. Add **8 g walnuts** and **5 g ground flaxseed** after reheating plus **1 orange** on the side. Optional **250 mL So Good High Protein Soy** (or an equivalent dairy-free calcium source elsewhere in the day). If this meal's lentils, tofu and greens are too fibre-heavy for your gut, **reduce the portion and increase fibre gradually**. Health is not maximising grams of fibre in one sitting.

## Safe freezing, storage, and portions

Follow the [NSW Food Authority leftovers advice](https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers): cool and refrigerate/freeze **within 2 hours**, split into shallow portions and keep fridge below **5°C**. Frozen leftovers are best used within **3 months** for its published guidance. In the fridge, rice/pasta leftovers should be used within **2 days** and other leftovers within **3 days**. Defrost in the fridge; use within **24 hours** of defrosting; reheat until steaming hot throughout and **never reheat rice more than once**. Use a working food thermometer when practical, especially with dense reheated portions. Cook **Batch A and B on different days** unless you have reliable cooling capacity for twenty sizeable lunches.

## Important reality checks

1. **No single recipe or ingredient can be identified as the objectively healthiest possible.** Whole-day/week patterns, tolerability, enjoyment, energy needs, budget and consistency matter far more than one nutrient.
2. **V4 is untested:** food yields, flavour, freezer texture, actual packaging availability, sodium and total calories depend on the actual items bought. Try a single portion before cooking all twenty if taste is uncertain.
3. **No absolute nutrient claims:** The product-label contribution alone is **27.8 g protein per sardine A serving, ~25.8 g per salmon A serving, ~22.4 g per tofu serving**, before adding legumes/grains/vegetables; exact totals require the full pack labels and cooked edible weights. Pureland tofu contributes a labelled **150 mg calcium per 125 g serving**. Don't assume the other foods close every daily nutrient gap.
4. **Dairy-free**, not automatically allergy-safe: check every label, cross-contact warnings, retailer substitutes and any shared cooking utensils. Fish and soy are known allergens; leafy greens are not a validated calcium substitute for fortified soy.
5. **V4 cost** refers to *the purchase basket*, not foods consumed: spare rice, quinoa, peas, oil, spices and other ingredients remain. See the [audited cost document](V4_SHOPPING_DUBBO_2026-10-16.md).
