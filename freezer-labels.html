Labels for your freezer.

Print and cut individual container labels for the original four V6 freezer lunches. Choose your actual preparation and freezing dates, not assumed dates. A printed label never proves a meal was chilled or stored safely.

Print using your browser, or choose “Save as PDF”. Labels list potentially relevant allergens by food family, but you must check actual product labels and cross-contact warnings. If any ingredient or cooked storage history is uncertain, do not assume the food is safe.

Source: NSW Food Authority leftover advice. These are date and handling prompts, not an expiry or nutrient certificate. Your entries remain in this browser and are not submitted to us.
