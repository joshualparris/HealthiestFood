## Savings using pantry shown in earlier photos — confirm actual remaining quantities

Do **not** automatically buy something just because the table says so: some of the following were shown in kitchen photos, but we **haven't confirmed how much remains**.

- Coles Wellness Road 100% quinoa: if ≥300 g remains **skip Woolworths quinoa** (~$5.75).
- Two Woolworths Essentials diced tomatoes 400 g each: if unopened with 100% tomato and no added salt, count 2 of 4 tins (~$4 saved at assumed price).
- Two sweet potatoes and two brown onions: use first after measuring peeled weights (possible ~$3.50 saved).
- Bertolli Light Tasting olive oil: acceptable unsaturated fat for roasting; EVOO optional improvement, **not a requirement** (up to ~$8.80 first-shop saving).
- Cumin, paprika **only if smoked**, turmeric, pepper, iodised salt: check enough amounts in jars; skip duplicates.
- One carton So Good High Protein **SOY** was photographed; **if it is still within date and sufficient** buy 4 rather than 5 cartons (save ~$3).
- Two existing 110 g Safcol Brisling sardine tins: **not counted as GF** because the Woolworths online listing reportedly says *may contain gluten/wheat/soy*. If strict GF, inspect your exact tin and choose suitable alternative rather than subtracting blindly. If not strictly avoiding gluten, decision is yours.
- Small pink salmon tins: do not count as matching the 210 g tin until drained contents are weighed. A smaller tin may not equal the ~166 g drained salmon needed for the 2 portions.
- Vitasoy Calci-Plus carton shown has **barley ingredients/gluten** and is not suitable for strict GF.
- Cashews visible: good food but **not the same ALA omega-3 source as walnuts**; keep for other meals rather than treating as nutritionally identical.

## Basket correctness checklist

- Fish: **12 sardine tins and 1 salmon 210 g tin**, not 12 salmon tins; sardines drained for 8 bowls, salmon drained for 2 bowls.
- Legumes: **2 chickpea + 1 black bean + 1 kidney bean**, all in A; **400 g dry lentils + 1 kg frozen baby peas** in B.
- Tofu: **3 × 450 g packs**, all in B; not 1 pack.
- Tomato tins: **4 × 400 g** across both batches.
- Frozen cauliflower/broccoli: **3 × 500 g** across both batches.
- Carrots: **800 g edible needed**, 1 kg bag is enough if no major waste.
- Oil: **110 mL across two batches**, 500 mL purchased.
- Walnuts: **150 g A + 100 g B = 250 g total**; buy **two 190 g** packs, not one.
- Pumpkin seeds/pepitas: **100 g total**; 300 g pack contains leftovers.
- Fruit: **10 oranges + 10 kiwifruit**, count orange bag.
- Soy milk (optional): **5 × 1 L**, enough for 250 mL each lunch.
- Cooking: two separate ten-bowl batches, **not 20 hot containers all at once**.
