# Podcasts and videos: an evidence map, not a library of endorsements

**Reviewed 17 October 2026.** HealthiestFood now has a **searchable, mobile-friendly library of 23 Australian audio/video resources** (16 individual ABC podcast episodes, 5 specific official FSANZ videos, and 2 Australian Heart Foundation webpages containing embedded videos). Every card links to its original publisher **and separately to authoritative research or dietary guidance**, and identifies what the media does not prove. Audio/video is **supplementary explanation, not proof**. The library neither embeds third-party players nor autoplays, so browsing the website does not contact YouTube/ABC until visitors deliberately open a link.

**Live (independently deployed):** https://joshualparris.github.io/HealthiestFood/#media
**Original production (when Vercel catches up):** https://healthiest-food.vercel.app/#media

## What media do NOT substantiate

For **exact Woolworths/Coles Dubbo prices and stock**, check the retailer checkout and local product availability. For **product-specific calcium, iodine, vitamin D, B12, sodium, fish drained mass and allergen labels**, read the current physical packaging. For **exact recipe nutrient estimates**, compare the linked [FSANZ nutrient database](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/science-data/food-nutrient-databases/afcd/data-files) against each ingredient with cooking yields, verified raw/drained quantities and actual consumed amounts. For **cooking times, freezer safety and cooked-food quality**, perform real cooking and cold-storage tests. For **claims about human lifespan**, no podcast, video or meal-score ranking proves that eating this app's recipes extends life by any particular amount.

**Iodine-specific**: no suitable verified single video/episode in the curated 23 establishes Australian bread iodisation requirements. Retain [FSANZ bread iodine fortification](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/food-fortification/iodine-fortification) and [Australian iodine NRV](https://www.eatforhealth.gov.au/nutrient-reference-values/nutrients/iodine) as the primary evidence. **Frozen berries for medically vulnerable people**: use direct [FSANZ vulnerable-populations guidance](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/prevention-of-foodborne-illness/Food-safety-for-vulnerable-populations) and actual pack instructions; a general kitchen-cleanliness video does not substitute for this.

## Verification standard and caveats

The named ABC episodes were opened/retrieved from actual ABC programme pages, with topic, presenter and episode date available. The official FSANZ video links were extracted from [FSANZ’s own food-safety-basics resource](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/prevention-of-foodborne-illness/food-safety-basics) where each is individually listed. The two Heart Foundation video collections are explicitly tagged *embedded webpage videos*; they are not presented as standalone YouTube files. No audio/video streams were independently listened to end-to-end and no episode's speaking claims were treated as primary science without the separate guidance link. Publisher content may change after the review date.

The collection is intentionally *outbound links*, not embedded players: no media downloads, copyright redistribution, YouTube autoplay, tracking cookies or new login is introduced into HealthiestFood. Recipe pages show at most three relevant links to avoid clutter. The main library provides podcast/video filters, topic filters and free-text search.

The website's **science, recipe and meal-planning claims should remain independently sourced with primary dietary guidance or original science** even when a linked media item becomes unavailable. This document is a claim-to-media editorial map, not a guarantee that every factual statement in all historical source archives is backed by a recording.
