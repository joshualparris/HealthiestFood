# V4 independent evidence and quality audit — 16 October 2026

See [V4 recipes](V4_RECIPES_2026-10-16.md) and [V4 Dubbo shopping table](V4_SHOPPING_DUBBO_2026-10-16.md). V3 remains saved separately, unchanged, for a reproducible comparison.

## Main conclusion

V4 is a **more carefully specified, nutritionally plausible variation**, **not** scientifically proven healthier than V3 or optimal for lifespan. Four concrete improvements are:
1. **Predictable calcium from a named tofu label** rather than assuming any tofu automatically supplies calcium; Coles Pureland high-protein lists 120 mg calcium and 17.9 g protein/100 g. The 125 g tofu allocation gives **150 mg labelled calcium and ~22.4 g protein per tofu lunch**; optional fortified soy adds another distinct calcium opportunity. [Source](https://www.coles.com.au/product/pureland-high-protein-firm-tofu-500g-6848120).
2. **No-added-salt black beans** rather than the **salt-added Woolworths black beans**: former [~8 mg sodium/100 g](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/663581), latter [~150 mg sodium/100 g](https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/907377), roughly **142 mg sodium per 100 g** less before rinsing effects. No-added-salt chickpeas and kidney beans are also specified.
3. **Fish arithmetic and diversity:** V4 schedules **four double-tin sardine bowls + four canned-salmon bowls + two tofu bowls** within A, while B has ten tofu/lentil bowls. Each week has **two fish lunches**, not an unproven assumption based on tin gross weight. More diverse flavour/protein rotation than V3's A-fish-only design, though whether it is subjectively tastier remains untested.
4. **Realistic small nut/seed amounts, labelled portions, exact cook methods and conservative price provenance.** V4 uses 8 g walnuts + 5 g ground flax per lunch, kept fresh, instead of adding unlimited fibre or a huge seed blend inside each freezer container. Preparing two 10-portion batches on different days reduces home refrigerator cooling load.

The most consequential unproven element is still **long-term adherence**: repeating enjoyable affordable meals for months is likely more important than a theoretical scoring bump.

**Error prevention:** Tofu, fish, lentils, canned beans and grains are specified by different mass conventions (wet/drained, dry uncooked, cooked finished), and those differences **must not be collapsed**. **Why not 100/100?** Energy needs vary, the menu doesn't supply an independently verified full-day iodine/B12/vitamin D/calcium profile, salmon/sardines aren't perfect protein foods for everyone, the meals haven't been tasted/frozen/tested in real conditions, and a high fibre serving may cause gut discomfort. A “perfect” score would be marketing rather than science.

## Australian evidence weighting and boundaries

**High confidence:** A broadly heart-healthy dietary pattern of vegetables, legumes, wholegrains, fruit, nuts, unsaturated fats and fish/plant proteins, as recommended by the [Australian Heart Foundation](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/healthy-living/healthy-eating/heart-healthy-eating-pattern) and [Australian Dietary Guidelines](https://www.eatforhealth.gov.au/food-essentials/five-food-groups/vegetables-and-legumes-beans). An Australian vegetable-group serve is commonly **about 75 g**, but a meal must not be optimised by blindly maximising number of vegetable serves. [Eat for Health serve sizes](https://www.eatforhealth.gov.au/food-essentials/how-much-do-we-need-each-day/serve-sizes).

**Good supportive evidence:** The [Heart Foundation's 2025 omega-3 guidance](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/healthy-living/healthy-eating/omega-3-omega-6-heart-health) recommends 2–3 weekly fish serves and plant ALA sources, including walnuts/ground flax. A large high-risk adult trial of Mediterranean-style diets supplemented with olive oil or nuts reported fewer major cardiovascular events **after a corrected 2018 reanalysis**; that is evidence about diet *patterns in that study population*, not proof that these particular lunch bowls extend lifespan. [PREDIMED republished NEJM 2018](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa1800389).

**Sodium:** The [Heart Foundation's May 2026 guide](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/sodium-and-salt-converter) advises no more than about **5 g salt / 2,000 mg sodium per day** for adults. V4 uses no-added-salt beans/tomatoes, citrus and herbs, but fish and soy drink still contribute sodium. **No precise per-bowl sodium total** is claimed without all package labels and actual edible weights.

**Food safety:** [NSW Food Authority leftovers guidance](https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/consumer/food-extra-care/leftovers) stresses shallow portions, into fridge or freezer within 2 hours, refrigerated rice/pasta ≤2 days, other leftovers ≤3 days, frozen leftovers ≤3 months and reheating thoroughly (especially rice, once). [FSANZ cooling and reheating](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/cooling-and-reheating-food) provides two-stage **business** benchmarks of 60°C to 21°C within 2 hours and 21°C to 5°C within another 4 hours, but those are **not a substitute** for sensible rapid shallow-portion home cooling.

## Practical tests before treating V4 as final

1. Check Coles Dubbo / Southlakes **Pureland 500 g stock**; record a photograph of the current ingredients/calcium panel *for personal use only*, not in this public repo.
2. Check four Woolworths no-added-salt bean tins and SPC tomatoes; update costs using selected store's actual shelf prices.
3. Make **one trial meal of each flavour** first. Adjust spice intensity, cooking time, container volume, fish aroma and freezer reheat texture.
4. Weigh actual **drained fish, cooked tofu, finished batch and individual containers**. Log true yield before publishing modelled nutrition numbers.
5. Record actual time it takes the **centre** of filled shallow containers to reach safe refrigeration temperatures; with only a crowded household fridge, split cooking into smaller sessions.
6. Optional: enter pack nutrition-panel figures and edible weights into a nutrient worksheet, then recalculate energy/protein/fibre/sodium for A-SARD, A-SALM, A-TOFU and B separately. Don't aggregate unlike protein variants into one misleading bowl number.
7. At follow-up, ask the key practical questions: were these actually cooked/eaten, affordable, appealing, and digestively comfortable? A meal that's not enjoyed is not a sustainable health strategy.

**Publication caveats:** V4 meals are **not confirmed cooked**, prices are **not Dubbo-checkout verified**, local individual product stock is **not confirmed**, product labels can change, health scores are **subjective**, and no medical/nutrient completeness or longevity outcome is established.
