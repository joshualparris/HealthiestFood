## 4.1 25 July 2025 - Rainbow Lentil & Veggie Bowls
Archive status Direct bulk/freezer request - detailed
Yield 6 freezer servings
Date coverage The later dated lunch plan used the six bowls on Sun 27 Jul, then Mon 28 Jul to Fri 1 Aug 2025; alternatively one bowl
could shift to Sun 3 Aug.
Evidence confidence High
84 / 100
Excellent vegetable and legume density. The main gaps are limited omega-3/healthy-fat
variety and no strong wholegrain component. The beef variant may improve protein but can
reduce the score if the mince is fatty.

Recovered recipe
- Red split lentils - 750 g dry - Broccoli - 600 g
- Cauliflower - 400 g - Zucchini - 300 g
- Capsicum - 450 g total - Garlic - 3 tsp or 3 cloves
- Olive oil - 15 mL - Turmeric - 1.5 tsp
- Lemon juice - 45 mL, added after reheating - Salt and pepper - to taste
Recovered method
- Cook lentils in about 2.4 L water with turmeric (the historical version also used 1 tsp salt) for 12-15 minutes.
- Steam broccoli, cauliflower and zucchini for about 4 minutes.
- Saute capsicum with garlic and olive oil for about 4 minutes.
- Divide into six containers; add lemon after reheating.

mince/onion/paprika quantities were not recoverable, so they are not invented here.
Best health upgrades if buying extra ingredients
- Add 100-150 g edamame or calcium-set tofu per serve across the batch, or rotate some serves with salmon/sardines.
- Add walnuts or ground flax for unsaturated fats.
- Keep added salt modest and use lemon, herbs, garlic and spices for flavour.
## 4.2 25 July 2025 - Cauliflower & Carrot Curry with Rice
Archive status Direct bulk/freezer request - detailed
Yield 6 freezer servings

Evidence confidence High

Health metric Score Interpretation

Healthy fats & omega-3 5/10 Moderate
Sodium control 7/10 Good

Portion balance & satiety 7/10 Good
Recovered recipe
- Brown rice or basmati - 600 g dry - Cauliflower - 800 g
- Carrots - 400 g - Thai red curry paste - 60 g
- Garlic - 3 tsp or 3 cloves - Light coconut cream - 375 mL
- Olive oil - 10 mL - Turmeric - 1 tsp
- Cumin - 1 tsp - Salt - 1/2 tsp
- Water - 1.2 L total
Recovered method
- Cook rice, slightly underdone for freezing.
- Saute garlic and curry paste in olive oil.
- Add carrot, cauliflower, cumin, turmeric and water; simmer about 10 minutes.
- Stir in coconut cream; simmer another 5 minutes.
- Portion curry and rice into six containers and freeze once safely chilled.
Best health upgrades if buying extra ingredients
- Use brown rice rather than basmati when practical.
- Add 2 cans no-added-salt chickpeas or 450-600 g calcium-set tofu to the batch.
- Add spinach or frozen peas.
- Replace roughly half the coconut cream with passata or unsalted stock/water.

Recovered recipe
- Red split lentils - 400 g dry - Carrots - 500 g
- Capsicum - 300 g - Zucchini - 200 g
- Onion - 200 g - Garlic - 4 tsp or 4 cloves
- Olive oil - 30 mL - Paprika - 1 tsp
- Cumin - 1 tsp - Vegetable stock or water - 2.4 L
Recovered method
- Roast carrot, capsicum, zucchini and onion with olive oil at 200 C for about 20 minutes.
- Simmer lentils with garlic, paprika and cumin for about 15 minutes.
